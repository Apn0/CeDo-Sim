## Form header

- Yellow diamond **"SAFETY FIRST"** sign top left of the body.
- Green banner: **"Dagelijkse controle voertuig"** (daily vehicle inspection).
- Identification fields (blank on this copy):
  - **"Heftruck nr:"** (forklift no.)
  - **"Machine nr :"** (machine no.)
  - **"Merlo nr:"** (Merlo telehandler no.)
  - **"Week nr"** (week no.)
  - **"Urenstand:"** (hour-meter reading)

## Grid layout

- Day columns: **Ma | Di | Wo | Do | Vr | Za | Zo** (Mon–Sun). Each day is subdivided into three shift columns: **OD | MD | ND** (Ochtenddienst / Middagdienst / Nachtdienst — morning / afternoon / night shift). Under each OD/MD/ND header sits a pair of small green/red tick cells (green = OK, red = niet OK).
- Left header block (green):
  - **"Tijdstip van controle door operator binnen 30 min na aanvang dienst"** (time of the check, performed by the operator within 30 minutes after the start of the shift).
  - **"Naam controleur"** (name of the person performing the check).

## Opmerkingen (remarks) box

Blue header **"Opmerkingen"**, followed by a red-gradient warning block (white text) and blank ruled lines for free-text remarks:

> **"Als de te controleren onderdelen met deze kleur afgevinkt worden met ROOD mag heftruck niet gebruikt worden. Veiligheid boven alles"**
> (If the items to be checked, [marked] with this colour, are ticked RED, the forklift may NOT be used. Safety above all.)

Reading: any check ticked red renders the vehicle unusable; the rows whose labels are printed in this same red gradient (8 Mast maximaal, 11 Remmen voetrem/handrem, 12 Dodemanspedaal/noodstop, 13 Claxon) are visually singled out as the critical safety functions.
