## Notes / open-question hits
- "**1e laserfilter**" — implies the housing holds **more than one filter disc** (a 2nd filter step likely follows on p.8 area / earlier steps); the filter unit has a **deur** (door) that swings open, filters can stick to it.
- Sealing discipline: flange face must be perfectly clean or the deur leaks melt — good failure mechanic for the sim (dirty flange => melt leak event).
- Confirms uitdraaischroef sits in a central bore through the filter door/flange; cleaned by pushing a tube through it from the front.
