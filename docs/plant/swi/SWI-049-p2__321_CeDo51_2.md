**EN gloss:**
- **Step 5 — Check the fences (Controleren hekwerken):** Check that the fences/guards are closed and that no workers are behind the fences.
- **Step 6 — Start the sorting line (Sorteerlijn opstarten):** Start the sorting line by: **1)** press the automatic button (it lights up); **2)** press the pre-heat button (voorverwarmknop); **3)** when the on-screen button turns green, press the green pushbutton. Then stay nearby a moment to watch for anything going wrong. Any fault/storing shows on the control panel — resolve it first before the sorting line is started.
