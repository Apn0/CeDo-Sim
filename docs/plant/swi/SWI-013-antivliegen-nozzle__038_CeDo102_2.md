## Notes / extracted facts
- The **balenpers** (bale press) has an **antivliegen** (anti-fly / fly-repellent spray) nozzle system fed from a compressed-air line — pest control on baled waste film. Nozzle is high up (hoogwerker required) and clog-prone (pierced open with welding wire).
- Cleaning consumables: **Dasty** (household degreaser brand) + nail brush; spare pierce-tool = welding wire; the kit is checked out from / returned to the **productie manager**.
- Battery-electric hoogwerker (returned "aan oplader") available in the plant — vehicle inventory data point for the sim (relates to Q23 vehicle checklist context: hoogwerker exists as plant vehicle).
