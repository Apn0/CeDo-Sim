## Notes / open-question hits
- New named part: **brakerplate(s)** — perforated breaker plate(s) sitting on a flange over a **bus** (bushing), fixed with 4 inbusbouten; removed with 2 threaded-rod pullers in dedicated recesses.
- **Setpoint verbatim: preheat oven at 200° C** for braker plates before fitting (thermal-shock avoidance). Heat-resistant gloves mandatory.
- Consumables: new pre-greased inbusboutjes each change; koperdraad poetsmatje (copper scouring pad) for flange cleaning.
- Confirms the site has a small parts **voorverwarmoven** near the extruder — distinct from the big burn-off oven for schraper/uitdraaischroef (p.8).
