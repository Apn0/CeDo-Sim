## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q4 (the "Rafter" 3B) / heetafslag:** Not the Rafter, but this documents the **heetafslag (hot die-face cutter / pelletizer head)** shared across **lijn 1, 3A and 3B**, and its clearing procedure for a **megakorrel** (giant melt lump forming in the die-face when melt solidifies). Tools: 10 mm socket (blades), 32 mm socket (central bolt), ratchet, plastic hammer.
- **Q7 / Q5 (pelletizing water):** Step 1 mentions **"Waterkolk zit vol met smelt"** — the heetafslag discharges into a **waterkolk** (water column / water vortex) which can fill with melt during a megakorrel event. Confirms a water-cooled die-face pelletizing loop at the heetafslag (relevant to granuleerwater / tankje-tussen-extruders questions).
- **Q22 / procedure timing:** Step 2 gives an explicit rule — after stopping the extruder screw, let the compactor keep running; **if clearing takes > 15 min, stop and empty the compactor**. (Real operational timing datum.)
- **Naming note:** This is **SWI-055** written as "PROD-SWI-55". Other SWI-055 scans (020_CeDo62_2, 058_CeDo63_2 = p4) exist; this is **page 1** of the same 4-page instruction, so SWI-055 = "Frame heetafslag verwijderen lijn 1 / 3A-3B", Issue 01, 12-10-2022, approved Peter Scheffer.
- **Cross-line confirmation:** heetafslag (die-face cutter) exists on **line 1 AND 3A AND 3B** — same machine type across these extrusion lines.
