## Notes / extracted facts (OPEN QUESTIONS hits)
- **Q1 (ZSS):** direct hit — "het water op de trommel is afkomstig van de **ZSS witte retour bak**" — ZSS is part of the water treatment feeding the lijn 1 voorwas trommel via a "witte retour bak" (white return tank) at **95–110 kuub/uur (m³/h)**. Doesn't spell out the acronym, but confirms ZSS has a white return tank and supplies wash water to lijn 1.
- **Water routing (Q5 adjacent):** ZSS witte retour bak → voorwas trommel lijn 1, nominal 95–110 m³/h.
- Lijn 1 has **two Kufferath units** (Kufferath 1 and 2 — Kufferath = German manufacturer GKD/Kufferath, likely dewatering screen presses), each with 3 automaat knoppen; operating pressure **~3,2 bar** set with reducer valve on the unit itself.
- Lijn 1 drying: **MAS drogers** (MAS = Austrian manufacturer of thermal dryers) with **cyclonen** and **verdeelwalsen** (distribution rollers) underneath.
- Start-up interlock lore: no water on voorwas trommel + running shredder = immediate blockage.
