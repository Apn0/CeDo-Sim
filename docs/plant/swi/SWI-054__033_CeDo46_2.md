## Notes / extracted facts
- The lijn 3 laserfilter has **TWO filter discs**: 1e laserfilter in the **frame** side, 2e laserfilter in the **deur** (door). Matches SWI-074's "vijzel II deurzijde" terminology — the filter unit has a frame side and a hinged door side.
- Internal parts named: **spanring** (clamping ring), **schraper** (scraper, rotates linksom/CCW, locates on a pin via a slot).
- Assembly relies on thermal expansion: hold disc ~1 minute against hot frame before pushing home; only plastic hammers allowed (no steel — protects filter discs).
