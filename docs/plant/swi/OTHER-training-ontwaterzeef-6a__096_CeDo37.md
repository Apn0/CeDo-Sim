## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q5 (water routing — Ontwaterzeef):** Confirms the **ontwaterzeef separates water from re-granulate**: water drains **through the screen into a bak (tub/tank)** below, while the **re-granulate continues toward the centrifuge**. This clarifies the Ontwaterzeef ↔ "tankje" relationship: the sieve drains into a collection tub, and solids proceed to the centrifuge. (The material handled is **re-granulaat** = ground/reground washed crumb.)
- **Q7 (tankje tussen extruders):** Adjacent-slide ghost text suggests a transport step to the **weegschaal (weighing scale)** after the ontwaterzeef, consistent with the crumb path centrifuge → weegschaal → extruder. (Not conclusive on the "tankje tussen extruders" itself.)
- Machine identity: an **inclined vibrating dewatering screen** feeding a **centrifuge** downstream. Material at this point is re-granulate crumb (dark grey), not film — relevant to Q24 (crumb density ~375-390 stage is downstream of this).
