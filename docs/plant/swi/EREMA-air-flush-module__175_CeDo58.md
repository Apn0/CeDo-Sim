## Slide — "Air Flush Module — Why necessary?"

**Text (verbatim):**
> "Air Flush Module — **Why necessary?**
> Repeated evaporation and condensation results in negative **sauna effect**:
> - Excess compactor motor load
> - Extra energy costs
> - Steam enters into extruder
> - Pellet quality issues
> - Increased plant maintenance"

**Embedded EREMA schematic:** cutaway of the **cutter-compactor + horizontal extruder screw** (screw shown in red with orange molten material in the compactor bowl). Grey arrows show **air/vapour flow**: moisture-laden air rising up and out of the top of the compactor (two upward arrows out the top vent), plus flow paths around the compactor throat into the extruder. Illustrates steam/vapour being flushed OUT of the compactor by the Air Flush Module rather than being repeatedly re-condensed ("sauna effect"). EREMA logo bottom-right and top-right.

## Notes / open-question hits
- **Machine spec (Q15/Q33 — compactor/extruder auxiliary):** documents the EREMA **Air Flush Module** — an option that vents moisture-laden air out of the cutter-compactor to prevent the **"sauna effect"** (repeated evaporation + condensation of moisture from wet flake). Without it: **excess compactor motor load, extra energy cost, steam entering the extruder, pellet-quality problems, more maintenance.**
- **Q20 (slow-running causes → remedies) — relevant:** "Excess compactor motor load" and "steam enters into extruder" from moisture/sauna effect is a concrete **cause of poor running / high motor load / pellet-quality drop**. Remedy per EREMA = the Air Flush Module (and, operationally, better upstream drying so flake enters the compactor drier). Ties to FORM-008 emphasis on drying (rows 6-7 vuilsnipper "lager=betere droging", rows 12/21/22 drying-related, and the dryer setpoints) — wet flake → sauna effect → compactor motor load → slow/unstable running. Sim linkage: infeed moisture ↑ ⇒ compactor motor load ↑ ⇒ throughput ↓ / pellet quality ↓.
- **Q24 (feed moisture/density) context:** reinforces that **wet feed is a first-order problem** for the compactor/extruder — moisture must be driven off upstream (wash-line dewatering + vuilsnipper drying) or it re-condenses in the compactor.
