## Notes / answers — Q28, Q18, sorting-line topology
- **The sorting line has exactly 2 Titech/Tomra units referenced together ("titech of tomra")** and **2 shredders**:
  - **Shredder 1 → control-panel value/tag "1030"**
  - **Shredder 2 → control-panel value/tag "4030"**
  (These "1030 / 4030" are panel identifiers or setpoint tags, not confirmed rpm — likely machine/asset numbers on the bedieningspaneel. Relevant to **Q18** shredder-rpm confusion: these are distinct machines with distinct panel IDs, so "lijn 1 32-50" vs "Shredder 1 35-60" rpm figures elsewhere may refer to different shredders, not a changed target.)
- **Bunker with doors (bunkerdeuren)** upstream of/around the shredders — confirms sorting line feeds from a bunker. Relevant to **Q16** "Bunker speed 200-800" context (there is a physical bunker at the sort line).
- Start-up gating: all maintenance done + all lockouts removed; both shredders in AUTO; Titech/Tomra fault-free; bunker doors closed.
- Cross-reference: pairs with **SWI-049** sortline start and the Titech calibration SWI-009.
