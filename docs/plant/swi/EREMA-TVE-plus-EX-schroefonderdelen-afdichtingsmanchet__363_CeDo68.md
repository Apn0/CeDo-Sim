## Section 2 — "TVE machine afdichtingsmanchet/bus" (sealing manchet/bush)

Body text (NL verbatim):
> "De afdichting bus moet zorgen voor de afdichting en dus voor de scheiding tussen ongefilterde en gefilterde smelt."
> ("The sealing bush must provide the seal and thus the separation between unfiltered and filtered melt.")

> "Dit deel van de schroef bevindt zich in het gebied van cilinderstuk 2 (VE-deel) dat zich onder het laserfilter bevindt of tussen de blokken van de zeefwisselaar op de TVE-machines."
> ("This part of the screw sits in the region of cylinder section 2 (VE-part) which is located under the laser filter, or between the blocks of the screen changer on the TVE machines.")

Bottom-left small schematic: screw section split into "rear part - before filter" (with labels "shallow groove" / "deep groove" via green arrows) and "front part - after filter".

Right-hand cross-section diagram (screen-changer block): color-coded flow —
- **Red = unfiltered melt** (ongefilterde smelt) entering from bottom-left / labeled top-left "unfiltered melt", bottom "unfiltered melt"
- **Blue/purple = filtered melt** (gefilterde smelt) exiting right / labeled "filtered melt"
- Top label: "screen-changer block - top / laserfilter"; bottom label: "screen-changer block - bottom"
- Arrows show melt passing up through the screen pack (hatched blue zone) then out.
