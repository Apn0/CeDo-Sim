**EN gloss:**
- **Step 74 — Laserfilter door:** Close the laserfilter door and put the bolts back in the door opening. Bolt threads must be coated/sprayed with **copper grease (kopervet)**.
- **Step 75 — Laserfilter bolts:** Tighten the bolts with a small air-hammer. Note: tighten **cross-wise / star pattern (kruislinks)** — per the clock-face diagram. **Socket Dop 36 (36 mm).**
- **Step 76 — Cordless nut-runner (accu moersleutel):** Hang the cordless impact wrench in its designated hanger (balancer).
- **Step 77 — Laserfilter bolts (final torque):** Re-tighten (final pass) the bolts with the cordless impact wrench. **Setting on 3** (torque/mode step 3 on the tool).

## Question hunt — VALUES
- **Bolt tightening pattern:** **kruislinks / star (cross) pattern** for the laserfilter door bolts, shown as a numbered clock-face diagram.
- **Socket:** **Dop 36** (36 mm) for door bolts (distinct from Dop 46 for the uitzetschroef on p23).
- **Cordless wrench final-pass setting:** **3** (tool torque step).
- **Consumable/prep rule:** door-bolt threads must be coated with **kopervet (copper grease)** before refitting (complements the "koperen ring replace each time" rule on p23).
- Reinforces LF 2-406 as the 24-page 2023 laserfilter-change procedure for line 3. Together with p23 (`325_CeDo160`): reassembly sequence = door bolts (Dop 36, cross-pattern, kopervet) → uitzetschroef (Dop 46, 500 Nm) → borgringen (+ new copper ring) → afschermkap.
