# EREMA reference — "De schrapers zijn belangrijk!" (scraper wear at screen change)

**Source scan:** `D:/drive-download-20251124T130330Z-1-001/178_CeDo73.pdf` (1 page)
**Doc type:** Not a SWI. EREMA maintenance reference on **schrapers (scrapers)** — the scraper blades on the melt-filter / laserfilter screen. Extracted exhaustively incl. photos.

## Body (verbatim)
> "Wie bespaart op schrapers, verliest geld! Het is essentieel dat de schrapers bij elke schermwissel worden vervangen. Naslijpen is mogelijk in geval van lichte slijtage. Volg de slijpinstructies in de documentatie."

**Gloss:** Whoever saves on scrapers loses money! It is essential that the scrapers are replaced at **every screen change (schermwissel)**. Re-grinding (naslijpen) is possible in case of light wear. Follow the grinding instructions in the documentation.

**Two labelled photos:**
1. **"Nieuwe schraper"** *(new scraper)* — Photo: a sharp, clean triangular/wedge-shaped steel scraper blade lying on a cutting-mat surface; crisp edge and flat faces.
2. **"Versleten schraper"** *(worn scraper)* — Photo: a scraper mounted on the curved filter disc/screen surface, showing pitting, scoring and rounded/eroded edges with two mounting bolt holes; heavily worn.

**Closing emphasis (verbatim):**
> "De schrapers zijn belangrijk!" *(The scrapers are important!)*

## Notes / open-question hits
- **Q15 (laserfilter) / melt-filter maintenance:** the **laserfilter (rotary-disc melt filter)** uses **schrapers (scraper blades)** that ride on the screen disc to clear retained contaminant off the sieve as it rotates. These MUST be replaced at **every screen change (schermwissel)**; light wear can be re-ground per the documentation. This is the wear part behind the FORM-008 "backflush/kopdruk" behaviour — worn scrapers → poor cleaning → rising head pressure.
- **Q20 (slow-running causes → remedies):** CONCRETE hit. **Cause: worn scrapers → melt filter not cleaned → kopdruk (head pressure) rises → more melt loss / backflush / slower stable output.** **Remedy: replace scrapers at every screen change (or re-grind if only lightly worn).** Ties directly to FORM-008 row 25 "Kopdruk kopfilter 3a — Hoger=backflush, lager=veel smelt" and row 27 "Kopfilters ≥1×/dienst wisselen anders loopt druk op." Sim modelling: scraper wear ↑ ⇒ filter cleaning efficiency ↓ ⇒ head pressure ↑ ⇒ throughput ↓ / backflush frequency ↑.
- **User's own project note (Laser filter):** matches the user's memory that CeDo runs a **Britas-style rotary-disc melt filter (laserfilter)** discharging lumps — the scrapers are what push retained contaminant ("lumps") off the disc into the discharge hose/lump_cart. This doc confirms scrapers as a first-class consumable/maintenance item in that filter.
