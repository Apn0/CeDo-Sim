## Notes / open-question hits
- **Q15 material (temperature zones around laserfilter):** the panel photo at the filter-change position shows zone temps **240 / 200 / 235 / 175 °C** side by side, together with **MD voor SF** (massadruk vóór smeltfilter = melt pressure before filter, reading 3 bar during change prep) and **MD na SF** (after filter, reading -6 bar). So the 175 °C and neighbouring zones sit AT the laserfilter block, with dedicated pressure sensors before and after the filter. The 245-250-255 zones (from the Extruderlijst) are not on this display — consistent with them being downstream (after-filter/die) zones on another screen.
- **Setpoint verbatim: melt pressure before laserfilter must be <= 10 bar ("gedaald tot minimaal 10 bar") before opening.** Extruder must be leeggedraaid first (referenced as "punt 2" of this SWI).
- **Q5/Q7 adjacent:** there is a **hete-lucht afvoer** flexible duct on top of the afvoerspindel — so the laserfilter discharge area has TWO flexible lines: hot-air exhaust on top, and the corrugated lump-discharge hose at the bottom (lump_cart). Good for modelling.
- New part names: **uitzetschroef / borgpen van de uitdraaischroef** (long greased locking pin), **afvoerspindel** (discharge spindle), **spindel om deur te openen** (door-opening spindle).
- Tool sizes verbatim: **dopmoer 36 en 46** (36 mm and 46 mm sockets), light + heavy air impact wrenches.
