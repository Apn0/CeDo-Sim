The plan is a grid of rectangular **halls** in two rows. Top row (with curved-roof/sawtooth roof symbols drawn above): **Hal 4, Hal 5, Hal 6, Hal 7, Hal 8**. Bottom row: **Hal 0, Hal 1, Hal 2, Hal 3**, with **Kantoren** (offices) along the lower-right. Entry **"Poort 4/5"** (gate 4/5) at top-left, and **"Magazijn"** (warehouse, printed vertically "Ma / gaz / ijn" = Magazijn) on the far left edge.
