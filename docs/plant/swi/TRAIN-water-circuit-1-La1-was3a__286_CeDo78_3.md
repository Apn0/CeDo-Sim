## Reading of the loop (material + water routing)
- Clean **water** enters at **La1 basin (1)** → **Pomp P1 (2)** pushes water forward → at **Doseerschroef (3)** film joins the water (water/folie) → through **Frictiewasser (4)** and **Glijgoot (5)** and **Pomp C1 (6)** the water carries the film (water/folie) → at **Frictiescheider M3 (7)** the **water and film are separated** → water passes through **Zandscheider (8)** (sand separator, removes grit) → back to **Waterbak La1 (1)**. Closed recirculating loop (center arrows = recirculation).

## Answers found
- **Q11 (LA1/LA2 meaning):** **La 1 = Waterbak La 1**, i.e. a water basin/tank that names **water circuit 1** of wash 3a. "La" = the tank designation for a wash-water circuit; La1 is circuit 1's basin (La2 = the second circuit's basin, per the companion "Water circuit 2 (La 2)" flipcharts). So LA1/LA2 = the two recirculating wash-water tanks/circuits of was 3a.
- **Q2 (block C1 on lijn 3A between Frictiewasser and Frictiescheider M3):** **C1 = Pomp C1** — position 6 in circuit 1, sitting between Glijgoot(5)/Frictiewasser(4) and Frictiescheider M3(7). So the "C1" block on the flow diagram between the frictiewasser and frictiescheider M3 is **Pump C1**, the pump that moves the water/film slurry from the friction washer stage into the Frictiescheider M3.
- **Q6 (Pomp zeefbocht):** Not on this circuit-1 chart. Circuit 1 pumps are P1 (clean water) and C1 (slurry). "Pomp zeefbocht" is a separate pump (sieve-bend pump) not shown here.
- **Q5 (water routing):** Circuit 1 (La1) is a closed loop: La1 basin → P1 → doseerschroef → frictiewasser → glijgoot → C1 → frictiescheider M3 (water/film split) → zandscheider → back to La1. Film is separated at M3; sand/grit removed at the zandscheider before water returns to the basin.
