## Notes / relevance to open questions — MANY HITS

- **Q2 (block C1) — CONFIRMS location.** "C1" is a discrete box **directly between "Frictiewasser (M1 en M2)" and "Frictiescheider M3"** in the main material flow. Combined with 115_CeDo8 ("6- Pomp C1"), **C1 = Pomp C1, the transfer pump moving the washed water/film slurry from the friction washer (M1/M2) to the friction separator M3.** ✔ Fully resolved. Evidence: box sequence "Frictiewasser (M1 en M2) → C1 → Frictiescheider M3".
- **Q3 (Is M11a shared by Mengsilo dosing screw AND infeed screw?) — STRONG EVIDENCE.** "Doseerschroef M11A" appears **twice**: once as the wash-line **infeed** dosing screw (Row 1, after Vuilsnipper silo) and once as the **Mengsilo** dosing screw (Row 3, under Mengsilo → "Doseerschroef M11a"). Also "Doseerschroef M11b" appears separately. This corroborates that **M11a labels the dosing/infeed screw that serves both the front-end infeed and the mengsilo dosing** (same machine number reused for the shared dosing-screw function). ✔ (relevant Q3). Evidence: two "M11A/M11a" boxes.
- **Q7/Q4/laserfilter (Q15).** The extruder box explicitly reads **"Extruder laserfilter vacuum"** — confirming the **laserfilter is integrated on the extruder** (with vacuum degassing), consistent with 108/112. Downstream: **Kopfilter & smeltslang** (head filter + melt hose), then **Ontwaterzeef en centrifuge**. ✔
- **Q7 ("Tankje tussen extruders").** Not explicitly named here; between extruder and downstream there is "Ontwaterzeef en centrifuge" and a melt hose ("smeltslang"). The dewater-sieve/centrifuge stage is where a small tank would sit. (No "tankje tussen extruders" box named on this slide.)
- **Q8 (Does lijn 3B have a bigbag station?).** This flow (3a machine numbers) ends at **"Bigbag station"** (branch off Weegschaal) and **"M5/L5 silo buiten"**. So the **3a/extruder train HAS a bigbag station**. (This slide is the 3a flow; it does not settle 3b specifically — but confirms a bigbag station exists on this line.) Evidence: box "Bigbag station".
- **Machine number inventory (huge, for plant map):** M11A/M11a & M11b (dosing screws), M1+M2 (frictiewasser), M3 (frictiescheider), M4 (frictiescheider), M8 (mechanische droger), m14 (verdeelwals), C1 (pomp), V1/V2/V2a/V3/V4 (ventilators/fans), plus Cycloon & windzifter, Thermische droger, Heaters, Mengsilo, Compactor, Extruder(+laserfilter+vacuum), Kopfilter & smeltslang, Ontwaterzeef & centrifuge, Weegschaal, Extruder silo, Compressor, Bigbag station, M5/L5 silo buiten.
- **Q19/Q24 (weighing).** "Weegschaal" (weighing scale) sits after the centrifuge and before the outside silo/bigbag — the point where product (crumb/pellet) is weighed. Relevant to stortgewicht measurement location.
