## Notes / relevance to open questions — MULTIPLE DIRECT HITS

- **Q11 (What do LA1/LA2 stand for?) — PARTIAL.** LA1 and LA2 are the labels of **Water circuit 1 and Water circuit 2** ("in wasinstallatie"). The slide gives them as circuit identifiers, not an expansion of the letters. So **La1 = wash-installation water circuit 1, La2 = wash-installation water circuit 2.** (Evidence: "Water circuit 1 (La1) in wasinstallatie / Water circuit 2 (La2) in wasinstallatie".) The literal letters "LA" are still unexpanded here — likely "Lucht/Loog/Lijn A" family; not stated. [unsure: exact expansion of the letters LA.]
- **Q12 (What does EOP stand for?) — PARTIAL/CONTEXT.** EOP here is tied to **Water circuit 3 = "blauwe tank water"** (the blue tank). So in the water-circuit context, **EOP = the blue-tank water circuit.** (Evidence: "Water circuit 3 (EOP) blauwe tank water".) Note: in other CeDo docs "EOP" appears as a drain destination ("Riool naar EOP") — EOP is likely the plant's **End-Of-Pipe** water treatment / effluent works, and circuit 3 (blue tank) is the EOP-treated water loop. [unsure: literal EOP = End Of Pipe — strongly implied by "Riool naar EOP" usage but not spelled out on this slide.]
- **Q10 / Q5 (blue tank).** Confirms **"blauwe tank" = the EOP water tank (Water circuit 3)** — treated/recycled process water, distinct from circuit 4 which is **fresh canal water ("vers kanaalwater", "Schoon")**. No volume or pump specs on this slide.
- **Q5 (water routing).** Four-circuit hierarchy established: La1 → La2 (dirtiest to cleaner wash circuits) → EOP blue-tank (treated recycle) → Schoon/fresh canal water (cleanest make-up), with overflow/waste going to **het riool** (sewer, → EOP per other docs). This is the master legend for interpreting the water-routing questions.
