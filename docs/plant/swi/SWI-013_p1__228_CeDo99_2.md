## Answers to open questions
- **Balenpers make/type (line 3):** The baler press on **lijn 3** is a **Bollegraaf HBC40** (clearly stencilled on the green machine in the step-3 photo). Relevant to Q8 (bigbag/baling station on 3B) and general line-3 equipment inventory.
- **Anti-fly / odorant system:** Line 3 baler press has an **"antivliegen nozzle"** that injects **geurmiddel/antigeurmiddel (odorant)** into the pressed bales — a fly-deterrent. Fed by a compressed-air-driven injection line; nozzle is swapped/cleaned periodically. This is a real, sim-worthy detail (bales get an anti-fly odorant treatment at the press).
- **Tools/consumables of note:** Dasty ontvetter (degreaser), lasdraad used to clean/clear the nozzle bore.
- **Cross-role:** replacement clean nozzle is kept in Peter Scheffer's office (procedural detail).
