## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q28 (TITECH/TOMRA):** SCADA control detail confirmed. Routing toggle **2040 = film to both sorting lines**; **2035 = all film diverted to the Tomra's** (so a Titech can be cleaned). An **'empty' / leegdraaien** toggle (0→1) empties the sorting belts before cleaning a Tomra; restored to 0 on start-up together with re-enabling the **dosering/bunker** (dosing feeder). Cleaning both Titech and Tomra share the same steps 3–5 (on missing page 2).
- Confirms upstream feed control is via a **"dosering/bunker"** on the HMI (bunker/doseerbunker feeding the sorters).
