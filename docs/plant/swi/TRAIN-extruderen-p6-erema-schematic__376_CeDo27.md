## Diagram — EREMA counter-current extruder (left→right flow)
Machine body labelled **EREMA** with **Counter Current®** logo (red hopper zone). Numbered blue callout bubbles:

1. **(top-left)** Roll/coil feed — a roller/reel feeding material via an inclined **infeed conveyor / geforceerde voeding** into the top of the machine. Material shown as chips/flakes tumbling down the incline.
2. **(red hopper, Counter Current® logo)** The **Counter Current®** cutter-compactor / preconditioning unit — orange-to-red gradient (heating/friction zone) where flakes are dried, heated and densified before entering the screw.
3. **(along the screw barrel)** The **extruderschroef** (extrusion screw) inside the barrel — long horizontal section with screw flighting shown.
4. **(two up-arrows above barrel)** Degassing / **ontgassing (vacuum)** zone — arrows indicate vapour/gas extraction upward from the melt.
5. **(red rotating element near barrel end)** The **laserfilter / smeltfilter** (melt filter, rotating disc filter) removing contaminants from the melt. [unsure: could also be depicted screen-changer; shown as red rotor]
6. **(far right, die head + cutter)** The **matrijs (die plate)** with the **heetafslag (hot-face die-face cutter)** — pelletizing head.
