## Facts extracted

- **Gevaarstape (hazard tape) = red and white**, means NO access at all. Access requires a **JSA** (Job Safety Analysis). Once controlled, hazard tape → replaced by **waarschuwingstape** (warning tape). Applied by hazard-identifier; **removed only by the Area Manager**.
- Colour-code / role hierarchy useful for a site-safety layer in the sim (barrier tape mechanic: red-white = hard block).
