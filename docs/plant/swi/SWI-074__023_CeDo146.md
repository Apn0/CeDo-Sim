## Cross-references / open-question notes
- **Laserfilter zeef cleaning route:** LF 2-406 screens are cleaned EXTERNALLY, explicitly NOT in the oven — so the Nordson oven (SWI-061) burns off other parts (e.g. heetafslag/other filter dies), not these laser screens. Useful for sim logistics (external cleaning = consumable/exchange pool of screens).
- Door opened with a stang (lever rod); consistent hot-work PPE chain across SWI-074 pages.
