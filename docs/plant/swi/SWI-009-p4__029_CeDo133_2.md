## Values / settings
- Calibration pass criterion: **spectrum = horizontale lijn op hoogte 1** after calibration.
- Scanner lamp switched on manually: draaiknop op "hand" at the **oranje kast** (orange control cabinet).
- Calibration reference: **barium** plate (white barium surface, never touch).

## Cross-references / open-question notes (Q28)
- **Q28 evidence:** each sorting machine has TWO NIR scanner heads — a **linkse en rechtse scanner** selectable with the 'scanner' button on the calibration screen (i.e. two scanners side by side over one belt, or the software addresses both machines' heads). The NIR housings are **blue** with a positioning sticker; the scanner lamp is controlled from the **orange cabinet** (TITECH orange).
- Calibration plate is TITECH-branded barium standard — consistent with older TITECH PolySort hardware on at least one machine.
