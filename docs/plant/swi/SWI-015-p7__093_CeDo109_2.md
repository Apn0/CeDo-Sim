## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q28 (TITECH/TOMRA):** There are **two Tomra sorters ("Tomra 1 en 2")**. Each has an **ejector bar of compressed-air nozzles** (the "ventielen"/valves). The HMI has Automatic / Manual / Cleaning modes plus a Maintenance menu with an "ejectors" test. Nozzle test = ~10 min per Tomra; each nozzle gets a puff of air and a **pressure difference flags a defective nozzle**. Defects are photographed and emailed to P.S / B.C / R.I / E.B. by 10:00 so the Technical Department (TD) can replace before line start.
