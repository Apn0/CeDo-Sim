## Notes / open-question hits
- **The burn-off oven is a NORDSON oven** (title verbatim) — this is the "oven" that SWI-054 p.8 sends the schraper + uitdraaischroef into ("schraper evenals de uitdraaischroef gaan de oven in"). Nordson makes vacuum pyrolysis cleaning ovens; the **vacuüm trolley** (step 2) confirms it is a vacuum-type cleaning oven with a wheeled parts trolley that slides ~2 m in/out of the vessel.
- HMI: **Siemens SIMATIC** touch panel; status text in English: **"Ready to switch on"**; "Heat" temperature readout must be ambient (example 17 °C) before opening — i.e. door interlock is procedural (temperature check), plus a **mechanische pal** and a handwheel-driven door.
- Newer doc generation: Issue 3, dated 19-12-2023, author "Lean Practitioner", approver **Peter Scheffer** (same approver as SWI-009 rev 04) — the 2023-era SWIs carry named approvers, unlike the 2022 "Manager Dept" ones.
