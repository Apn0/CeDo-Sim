## Answers to open questions
- **Q30 / Q16 (units):** "Definities: KW=KiloWatt, aangegeven vermogen" — confirms **compactor/extruder power values on these SWIs are in kW** (aangegeven vermogen = indicated power). Compactor shutdown threshold **60-50 kW**.
- **Q15/temperature:** compactor temperature setpoint dropped **123 °C → 118 °C** near end of run to avoid a melt rim — relevant compactor temp band (distinct from extruder zone temps).
- **Q13/Q22 (compactor 3a/3b):** title explicitly "Extruder 3a/3b" — one SWI covers both extruders 3a and 3b (supports that references to compactor "3a" vs "3B" are the two parallel lines, relevant to FORM-008 row 22 duplicate-compactor question Q13).
