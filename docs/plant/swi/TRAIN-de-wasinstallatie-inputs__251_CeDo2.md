## Answers hunted
- **Q12 (EOP meaning):** "Water vanuit EOP" = "**gerecycled water, € goedkoop**" — so **EOP supplies recycled (cheap) process water** to the wash installations, as opposed to fresh canal water which is more expensive. Confirms EOP is the recycled-water source/treatment. Doc 251_CeDo2. (Complements 244_CeDo126 "EOP water zuivering system" and earlier "Riool naar EOP".) [Literal expansion of EOP still not spelled out; function = recycled-water supply/treatment.]
- **Q5 (water routing):** Two clean-water feeds to wash: (1) **EOP recycled water** — cheap, and (2) **vers kanaalwater** — from the canal, more expensive. Operators/logic prefer EOP recycled water for cost. Doc 251_CeDo2. Useful for the sim's water-cost economics.
- **Hydrauliek → tandwielpomp:** hydraulics drive a **gear pump** (tandwielpomp) — likely the melt/gear pump; hydraulic drive noted.
- **Perslucht → kleppen; Kleppen → water level:** compressed air opens valves; valves maintain water level — utility dependency chain for the wash tanks.
- Grondstof = **LDPE folie** confirmed as the wash-line feedstock (matches user background: LDPE film recycling).
