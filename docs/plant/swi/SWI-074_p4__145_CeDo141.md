## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q15 / laserfilter (KEY EVIDENCE):** There are (at least) **THREE laserfilters** at the plant with two distinct sizes/types:
  - **LF 2 (tag 406)** — the LARGE new-type filter documented here; twin discharge augers (I = E-motor/drive side with oval drive coupling; II = door side); a **pair of round filter screens** (item 17) + a single **spanring/clamp ring** (item 16); a **new-type triangular scraper** with a central toothed drive.
  - **laserfilter 3a and 3b** — SMALLER (explicit quote: *"Afmetingen zijn groter dan laserfilter 3a en 3b"* = LF 2's dimensions are larger than those of laserfilter 3a and 3b). This confirms lines **3A and 3B each have their own (smaller) laserfilter**, and LF 2 (=406) is a separate, larger unit (likely line 1 / the big extruder line).
  - Scraper geometry: **Reuleaux-triangle plate, central toothed drive** — matches a rotary-scraped disc melt filter. Directly useful for the user's laser-filter project geometry.
  - Assembly detail: auger ends get **kopervet (copper grease)**; drive coupling is **oval**.
