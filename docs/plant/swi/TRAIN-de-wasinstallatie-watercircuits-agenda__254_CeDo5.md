- **Q11 (LA1/LA2 meaning):** **DEFINITIVELY RESOLVED.** "Water circuit 1 (La1)" and "Water circuit 2 (La2)" — **La1 = water circuit 1, La2 = water circuit 2** in the wash installation. Doc 254_CeDo5.
- **Q12 (EOP meaning):** **RESOLVED.** "Water circuit 3 (EOP) blauwe tank water" — **EOP is water circuit 3, i.e. the blue-tank (blauwe tank) recycled-water circuit.** Combined with 251_CeDo2 ("Water vanuit EOP = gerecycled water, goedkoop") and 244_CeDo126 ("EOP water zuivering system"), **EOP = the recycled/treated process-water circuit that fills the blauwe tank**. Doc 254_CeDo5. [Literal initialism expansion of "EOP" still not printed, but its role = circuit 3 / blauwe-tank recycled water is now fully pinned.]
