## Body text (verbatim, Dutch)

> "De folie komt via de frictieschroef M3 en inlaatpaddel de flotatietank binnen.
> Alles met SG van >1.0 zal naar de bodem zinken en afgevoerd worden naar een afval container.
> De schoepen aan het oppervlaktewater transporteren de folie voorwaarts.
> De uitdraaischroef van flotatietank zorgt ervoor dat folie uit de tank getransporteerd wordt."

English gloss:
- Film enters the flotation tank via the **frictieschroef M3** (friction screw M3 / Frictiescheider M3) and the **inlaatpaddel** (inlet paddle).
- Everything with **SG > 1.0** (specific gravity above 1.0, i.e. denser than water) sinks to the bottom and is discharged to a **waste container** (afval container).
- The **schoepen** (surface paddles) transport the floating film forward.
- The **uitdraaischroef** (discharge screw) transports the film out of the tank.

## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q1 / Q2 / Q5 (flotation tank, water routing, C1 block, LA naming):** Major clarity on the 3A wash sequence. After **Frictiescheider M3** ("frictieschroef M3"), film enters the **LA3 flotatie tank** via an inlet paddle. Separation is by density: **SG > 1.0 sinks** and goes to a **waste container**; film (SG < 1.0) floats, is pushed forward by surface **schoepen**, and is drawn out by the **uitdraaischroef**. So the flotation tank is the sink/float density separator downstream of M3. Verbatim tank label: "LA3 flotatie tank".
- **Q11 (what do LA1/LA2 stand for):** Strong evidence — the flotation tank is labelled **"LA3"**, and other docs reference **LA1/LA2**. "LA" is the tag prefix for the flotation/wash tanks (**LA = "flotatie tank" series**, numbered LA1/LA2/LA3). This slide fixes **LA3 = the flotation tank on line 3A**, fed from M3. (LA likely a wash-line tank designation; LA1 and LA2 are the earlier tanks in the same 3A wash train — consistent with the water-circuit slides TRAIN-water-circuit-1-La1-was3a / -2-La2-was3a.)
- **Q5 (drains / waste routing):** Confirms heavy sinkers from the flotation tank are sent to an **afval container** (waste container / skip), not back into the water loop.
- **Q2 (block C1 between Frictiewasser M1/M2 and Frictiescheider M3):** Still not explicitly named "C1," but confirms the M-series ordering: Frictiewasser (M1/M2) → **Frictiescheider M3** → **LA3 flotatietank**. The "toevoer vanuit M3" arrow places the flotation tank immediately after M3.
