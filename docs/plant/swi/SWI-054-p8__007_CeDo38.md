## Notes / open-question hits (laser filter project relevance)
- Laserfilter internals named verbatim: **laserfilter** (the filter disc), **schraper** (spiral scraper in a **ring**), **uitdraaischroef** (discharge screw — this is the screw that pushes contaminant "lumps" out, i.e. what feeds the corrugated bottom hose into the lump_cart), **spanring** (clamp ring).
- Cleaning logistics: dirty filters collected in a separate container and **cleaned externally**; schraper + uitdraaischroef are cleaned in an **oven** (pyrolysis/burn-off oven on site).
- Tools: plamuurmes (putty knife), kleine koevoet (small crowbar).
- The full 17-page SWI-054 covers the entire filter change; steps 1-27 and 32+ are on pages not in this file.
