## Notes for sim / open questions
- Confirms Q4-adjacent laser-filter detail: LF 2-406 uses removable **brakerplates** (breaker plates) held by bolts; threaded rods (draadeinden) used as fall-arrest/handling aids during change. Cleaning of the flange seat with wire brush ("als nieuw") is a quality gate.
