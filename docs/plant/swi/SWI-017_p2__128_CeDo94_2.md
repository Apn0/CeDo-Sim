## Values / settings captured (verbatim)
- Step 4: compactor at **40 KW** → schuif **100% open**.
- Step 5: compactor at **5 KW** → extruder to **afkeur/bigbag station**.
- Step 6: compactor at **0 KW** → stop compactor.
- Step 7: laserfilter pressure **< 5 bar** → may stop extruder.
- Photo readouts (step 7 HMI): temp zones **240 °C**, **200 °C**, **[unsure: 235]** °C; **KW 0**.

## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q8 (bigbag station on 3B):** YES — evidence. Step 5 says the extruder is switched to the **"afkeur/bigbag station"** during shutdown of Extruder **3a/3b**. Since this single SWI covers both 3a and 3b, a **bigbag/afkeur station exists on the 3a/3b extruder line** (used for off-spec/purge material). Verbatim: "zet je de extruder op afkeur/bigbag station."
- **Q15 (extruder temp zones, laserfilter):** Confirms the **laserfilter has a melt-pressure gauge** (bar) used as a shutdown criterion (**< 5 bar → stop**). Step-7 HMI shows temp zones **240 °C / 200 °C / ~235 °C** on 3a/3b — real extruder zone values on this line. Confirms the laserfilter sits in the melt stream with its own pressure readout.
- **Q30 (units — power_CC / hoofdmotor):** The **compactor load is read in KW** (kilowatts) on the HMI (40 KW → 5 KW → 0 KW during rundown). So compactor / "CC" power is in **kW**. This directly answers the power_CC unit as **kW**.
- **Q20 (slow-running causes → remedies):** Procedural remedy captured: to empty the extruder cleanly you stage the compactor down (40→5→0 KW) opening the schuif to 100%, divert to afkeur/bigbag, and stop the extruder only once laserfilter pressure < 5 bar — prevents fines/poorly-degassed product reaching the silo.
- **Quality rule (green diamond):** Diverting to afkeur/bigbag at 5 KW protects the outdoor silo from **fines and poorly-degassed (ontgast) product** — reinforces that the outdoor silo holds finished good product (Q27).
