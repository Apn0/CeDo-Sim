- **Step 11 — Heetafslag (die-face / hot-cut):** Replace the die-face (hot-cut) blades if necessary. Wear leather gloves — the blades are sharp!
- **Step 12 — Compactor temperatuur:** Set the compactor temperature setting back to **123 degrees** (°C). (i.e. after run-empty/stop, restore the compactor temp setpoint to 123 °C.)
