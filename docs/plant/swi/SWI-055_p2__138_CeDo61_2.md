## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q5 / general pelletiser detail:** The "heetafslag" (hot die-face pelletiser cutter) has a **4-arm spider blade holder** with removable **messen (blades)** and a **central bolt with LEFT-HAND thread** (loosen clockwise, socket 32). Removing blades first makes maintenance safe. Confirms the die-face cutter is common to **line 1 and lines 3A/3B** ("lijn 1 3A-3B" in the title). Relates to Q15/laserfilter-adjacent pelletising and to messen/matrijsplaat wear (see existing digest `messen-matrijsplaat-slijtage__066_CeDo82.md`).
