## Values / facts
- The pers–container interface has: **buizen** (closing tubes pushed forward by the press to seal the container mouth), **pinnen** + **vergrendel pinnetjes** (pin-in-pin locking, pins chained to the machine), **grijpklauwen** (grip claws that clamp the container; they drop for the sensors before the container may travel), and a **zeil** (tarp) on the container.
- On decoupling, the container shifts **20 cm** backwards.
- Interlock logic: container cannot move unless grip claws have dropped in front of the sensors — sim-able failure state (container refuses to ride).
