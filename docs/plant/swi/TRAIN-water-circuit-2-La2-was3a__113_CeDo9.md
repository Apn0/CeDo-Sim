## Cycle diagram (as drawn) — clockwise ring around center "La2"

A blue ring with 5 yellow dots (stations 1–5) and two curved recycle arrows in the center labeled **"La2"**. Around the ring, each station is annotated with what flows there:

- **Station 1 (top): "water"** — Waterbak La2 (water)
- **Station 2 (right): "water"** — Pomp P2 (water)
- **Station 3 (lower-right): "water/folie"** — Flotatietank (water + film together)
- **Station 4 (lower-left): "water/folie gescheiden van mekaar"** — Ontwaterschroef M3 (water and film **separated from each other**)
- **Station 5 (upper-left): "water"** — Glijgoot (water)

**Flow (clockwise):** Waterbak La2 (1, clean water) → Pomp P2 (2, pumps water) → Flotatietank (3, water+film mixed, flotation separation) → Ontwaterschroef M3 (4, water and film are separated / dewatered) → Glijgoot (5, water returns) → back to Waterbak La2 (1). The center arrows show it is a **closed recirculating loop** of the La2 water.
