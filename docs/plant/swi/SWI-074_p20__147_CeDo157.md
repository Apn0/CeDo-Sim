## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q15 / laserfilter:** Re-assembly sequence detail — the triangular scraper is keyed by its **central toothing/splines and fits only one way** onto the ring gear; a **new copper sealing ring** is mandatory each change; two filter screens ("eerste filter" p19, "tweede filter" here) are bi-metal (thermal-expansion retained) and tapped home. Confirms the twin-screen, single-scraper, ring-gear-driven architecture of the large (LF 2 / 406) melt filter.
