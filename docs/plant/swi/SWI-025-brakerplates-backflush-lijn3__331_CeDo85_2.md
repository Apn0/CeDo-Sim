- Adds detail on the **line-3 backflush filter** breaker plates: held by **3 bolts** loosened with a large flat screwdriver; hot-work — heat-resistant glove required on the holding hand; clean bolts + plate with wire brush and putty knife.
- No direct answers to numbered Q1-Q33 (this is a maintenance SWI). Distinguishes two filter types on line 3: **laserfilter (LF 2-406, SWI-074)** vs **backflush filter (SWI-025)** — relevant background for Q4/Q15 filter modelling.
