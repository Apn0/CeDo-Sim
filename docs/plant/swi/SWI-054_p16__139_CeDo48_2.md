## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q15 (laserfilter):** Confirms the **laserfilter is on line 3** and is a bolted round screen-changer housing with a **buitenste afvoervijzel (outer discharge auger)** that carries away the filtered-out contaminant ("lumps") — mechanically consistent with the Britas-style rotary melt filter. Torque-up of the housing bolts uses a modified pneumatic impact set to a fixed torque; a locking-pin (borgpen) indexes the discharge auger. The change procedure is long (17 pages). No specific temperature-zone or before/after-filter data on this page.
