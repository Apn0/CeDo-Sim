**EN gloss:**
- **Step 82 — Laserfilter set-screw (uitzetschroef):** Screw the set/jacking screw into discharge-auger II. **Socket size 46 (Dop 46).**
- **Step 83 — Tighten the set-screw:** Use the special torque wrench **already preset to 500 Nm**. Bolt grade **8.8**, target torque **MA = 500 Nm**.
- **Step 84 — Locking rings (borgringen):** Put the 2 locking rings back. The locking rings do not need to be replaced every time, **but the copper ring does** (koperen ring = replace each time).
- **Step 85 — Laserfilter cap/hood (Laserfilterkap):** Rotate the protective hood so it is first directly opposite the door. **Mind the cabling.**
