## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q15 / laserfilter procedure structure:** The SWI has a **decision point at step 53**: *filter-only* change skips to step 54; *filter + breaker-plate* change branches back to step 48 (note the non-linear numbering — the breaker-plate module physically appears earlier in the page order but is optional). The **breaker plates are fixed with 4 Allen bolts** plus threaded-rod holes. The filter **door ("deur")** is a hinged perforated plate cleaned both sides. Confirms breaker plates are a swappable wear item behind the filter screen.
