## Water routing (connector lines) — Q5 answers for 3A
- **P1 → Frictiewasser:** pump P1 supplies wash water to the friction washer (confirmed by 252_CeDo13).
- **C1 → LA1 / Zandscheider naar LA1:** C1 pump discharge and the sand separator both route toward **LA1** (first flotation), matching 252_CeDo13 "C1 pomp richting LA3 1e flotatie" — here labeled LA1 for line 3A's first flotation.
- **P2 / LA2 ↔ Flotatie tank:** P2 pump and LA2 tank service the flotation-tank / dewatering side (second flotation stage).
- **ZSS → Blauwe tank; Vers kanaalwater → Blauwe tank:** the blue tank is charged by ZSS-treated water + fresh canal make-up.
- **Blauwe tank → Pomp was 3a and Pomp was 4:** blue tank distributes clean wash water to the wash pumps (3a wash and wash-4/frictiescheider M4 area).
- **Blauwe vat + Pomp:** a separate small blue vat with pump near the extruder/compactor (extruder/laserfilter water side).
- **Tankje tussen extruders + Pomp:** a small tank between the extruders with its own pump, serving the ontwaterzeef/pelletizing water (granulation dewatering loop).
- Long purple return lines run from the pelletizing area (Ontwaterzeef/Centrifuge) back to ZSS/Blauwe tank (process water recovery).
