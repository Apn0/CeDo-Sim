## Branch logic captured (step 34)
- Two optional "Extra module" sub-procedures:
  1. **Binnenste afvoervijzel (uitdraaischroef) wisselen** — change inner discharge/unscrew screw → **start at handeling 35**.
  2. **Brakerplates laserfilter wisselen** — change laser-filter breaker plates → **start at handeling 41**.
- If NO extra module: skip to **handeling 48**.
