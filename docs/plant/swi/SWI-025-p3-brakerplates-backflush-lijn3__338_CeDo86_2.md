## Answers to open questions
- **Backflush filter (lijn 3) internals:** confirms the line-3 backflush filter uses a **piston** with **2 breaker plates (left + right)**; the piston top has 2 holes used as pry points (hole nearest base = right plate, outer hole = left plate). Cleaned plates go into a **vacuum oven** (vacuüm oven) — same cleaning route noted for laserfilter screens elsewhere.
- **Doc dating:** SWI-025-00 issued **22 September 2021**, approved by Site Director — one of the older procedures.
- Distinguishes the **backflush filter (piston + 2 brakerplates, SWI-025)** from the **laserfilter LF 2-406 (SWI-074)** — both on line 3. Relevant to Q4 (laserfilter make/type) and filter-modelling: line 3 has BOTH a backflush filter and a laser filter.
- No direct answers to other numbered questions Q1-Q3, Q5-Q24, Q26-Q33.
