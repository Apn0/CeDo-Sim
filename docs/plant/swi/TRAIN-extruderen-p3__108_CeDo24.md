## Notes / relevance to open questions

- **Q15 (laserfilter location) — DIRECT HIT.** The caption states the **laserfilter sits "ter hoogte van nr 3"** — i.e. at the position of callout 3, which is the **extruder screw/barrel section itself (early/mid-barrel, downstream of the compactor and upstream of the vent zone 4 and the screen changer 5)**. It is "niet afgebeeld" (not drawn) on the training diagram. This places the laserfilter **within/along the extruder barrel**, distinct from the screen changer at (5). Evidence: "Ter hoogte van nr 3 zit er nog een laserfilter, niet afgebeeld."
- Confirms the **extruder screw has multiple temperature zones** ("een aantal zones die nog besproken worden") — the numbered zone temperatures (Q15: 200-235-175-240-245-250-255 °C) map onto these barrel zones. This slide does not yet list the values (deferred to a later slide "die nog besproken worden").
- No numeric setpoints on this slide.
