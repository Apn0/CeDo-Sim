**EN gloss (step 11 — "MAS ventilatoren" / MAS fans):** Check that the 2 fans after the MAS dryers are blowing; the film strips at the top of the cyclone above the silo will then move.
- "MAS drogers" = MAS mechanical/centrifugal dryers on the wash-to-dry transition; the two fans provide the pneumatic transport airflow that lifts dried film up through the cyclone into the silo. The telltale is the folie strips fluttering at the cyclone top.
