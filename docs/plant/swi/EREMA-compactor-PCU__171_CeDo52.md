## Notes / open-question hits
- **PCU expansion (relevant to Q30 / EREMA nomenclature and the 166_CeDo69 temperature sheet):** CONFIRMED **PCU = Pre-Conditioning Unit** = the **Compactor / verdichter** = "het hart van het Erema systeem." Formerly called "verdichter." This resolves the "PCU" zone label seen on the EREMA temperature profile sheet (166_CeDo69) — the PCU temperature is the **compactor** temperature (which FORM-008 sets to 123 °C, rows 19-20).
- **Machine-function reference for the sim:** the compactor performs 7 functions in one step — **cuts, homogenises, heats, degasses, compacts, buffers, doses**. This is the cutter-compactor (INTAREMA front end) that feeds the extruder; useful for sim tooltip/mechanic descriptions of the compactor stage.
- Ties the FORM-008 "Compactor 3a/3b temperatuur 123 °C — hoger=plastificeren" setpoint to the PCU (pre-conditioning) stage: the compactor heats/softens flake WITHOUT melting (plastifying is undesirable = too hot).
- Companion to EREMA sheets 166/167/169 — all from the same EREMA training/manual binder.
