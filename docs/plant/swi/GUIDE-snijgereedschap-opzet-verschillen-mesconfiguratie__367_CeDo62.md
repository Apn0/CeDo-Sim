**Heading (bottom-left as scanned, i.e. real top-left): "Opzet verschillen"** (setup / configuration differences)

Three disc photos across the row (grey cutter/rotor discs with colored blade inserts):
1. **Grey disc with many radial GREEN blades** (roughly 12 straight radial cutting blades arranged like spokes) — labeled below (de-mirrored): **"Prestaties bij lage snelheid en naslijpen"** (performance at low speed and re-grinding/re-sharpening). A separate small green blade element floats above it.
2. **Middle disc:** grey disc with two large curved dark scoop/vane cutouts (a low-blade-count aggressive scoop configuration).
3. **Right disc:** grey disc with a few (≈3) YELLOW blade segments in a spiral/curved arrangement — labeled below (de-mirrored): **"Snijden met hoge snelheid (folie)"** (cutting at high speed — film/folie).

**Interpretation:** the disc blade layout is chosen for the duty:
- Many green radial blades = good for **low speed + easy re-sharpening (naslijpen)**.
- Few yellow curved blades = optimized for **high-speed cutting of film (folie)**.
