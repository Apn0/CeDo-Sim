## Notes / answers — Q28 (TITECH / TOMRA)
- **Multiple scanners confirmed:** calibration is run **per scanner** ("continue with the other scanners"). The NIR unit has more than one scanner/sensor head.
- **Scanner lamp** is switched via a **rotary knob on an "orange cabinet" (oranje kast)** and must be returned to **AUTO** after calibration. (The "oranje kast" is a named field asset — matches the "oranje kast" referenced in p3-era instructions.)
- **Tomra transport belt is LOTO-secured** (padlock on rotary safety switch) during the plate-handling steps — the belt under the NIR sorter is the "transportband".
- Row mis-numbered "4" instead of 14 in the source — authoring error, preserved.
- Still does NOT enumerate sorting programs / ejected fractions / EM-sensor / belt width. This SWI is purely the calibration procedure.
- Pairs with p3 (154_CeDo132) — login "4321", flat-spectrum-at-height-1 acceptance criterion.
