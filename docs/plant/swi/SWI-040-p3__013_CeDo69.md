## Notes / open-question hits
- **Machine make/type: Lijn 3 shredder 2 is a LINDNER KOMET 2800** [unsure on "2800": branding photo partially legible]. First hard manufacturer ID for the lijn 3 shredders. (Relevant to Q18 — lijn 1 shredder vs lijn 3 shredders are distinct machines; the Komet is a Lindner single-shaft shredder.)
- Safety design: rotor jog = **tweehandsbediening** (two-hand control) — matches SWI-034-p2 note (two-hand jog forward/reverse).
- Shredder 2 has a **zeef** (screen basket) under the rotor accessible via rear doors opened with a moertacker — crumb size is screen-defined; cleaning the zeef is part of the open procedure.
- End-state of SWI: "Shredder is klaar voor technisch onderhoud" — this SWI is the production-side handover to TD (technische dienst).
