The **balenpers** (bale press) on line 3 produces reject/off-spec bales that are transported outside by forklift. Relevant to bale bulk-density and reject-material handling.
- **Reject handling detail:** "reject balen" — off-spec material on line 3 is baled and moved outside; frequency of wire inspection is pegged to reject throughput (every 3 bales).
