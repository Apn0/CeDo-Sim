## Diagram — flow arrows / routing (as drawn)

- **Blauwe tank (central) is the distribution hub.** Thick arrows FROM the Blauwe tank feed OUT to the wash consumers on the right and to process users.
- **Kanaalwater → Blauwe tank** (fresh canal water tops up the blue tank; Kanaalwater also feeds the extruder cooling/vacuum boxes).
- **Blauwe tank → Wash line 1**, **→ Was 3a LA2 & rest van was 3a**, **→ Was 3a vuilsnippersilo**, **→ Was 3b vuilsnipper silo**, **→ Was 3b flotatie tank**, **→ Was 3b rafter** (blue tank supplies process water to lijn 1, 3a and 3b wash consumers — matches subtitle "voegt water toe aan de wasinstallaties").
- **Blauwe tank → Extruder 3a/3b/lijn1 koelwater van warmte wisselaars** and **→ ... vacuum en centrifuge water** (blue-tank water also serves extruder heat-exchanger cooling and vacuum/centrifuge water).
- **ZSS proces** sits top-left; arrows route between ZSS proces and the Blauwe tank / top header line (ZSS supplies/receives water at the top of the loop). A long top bus-line connects ZSS proces across to Wash line 1 / Put & pomp was 1.
- **EOP proces** (lower-left) ← receives return; **EOP proces ↔ Blauwe tank** (thick arrow) and **EOP proces → ZSS proces** up the left side. EOP is the treatment stage that regenerates blue-tank water.
- **Right column consumers → Riool naar EOP**: Was 3a vuilsnippersilo, Was 3b vuilsnipper silo, Was 3b flotatie tank, Was 3b rafter, Was 3a LA2 all drain to **"Riool naar EOP"** (sewer to EOP), and **Put & pomp was 1** ties into the top return. Riool naar EOP → EOP proces closes the loop (used water goes to EOP treatment, then back to blue tank).

**Net loop:** Kanaalwater (fresh) + EOP-treated water → **Blauwe tank** → wash consumers (lijn 1 / 3a / 3b) + extruder cooling/vacuum/centrifuge water → dirty return via silos/flotation/rafter → **Riool naar EOP** → **EOP proces** → (**ZSS proces**) → back to Blauwe tank.

Evidence: box "ZSS proces" wired into the blue-tank ↔ EOP loop.
