## Notes / answers
- **Nordson oven = OEM burn-off oven** (brand Nordson). Terminology mapping is useful: the "vacuumpot" (step 18–20 on p6) is the **Primary Trap**; the "waterfilter" (steps 21–22 on p6) is the **Secondary Trap Basket**; interior = **Oven Chamber**. All serviced every cleaning cycle.
- Oven has **two drains (afvoeren)** on the chamber floor that must stay open; a floor heating element that must be clear of melt.
- Oven door sealed via a **handwheel**.
- Confirms this is the plant's polymer burn-off station for de-coking metal parts (filter discs, spanringen, breaker plates, dies) — supports Q15/Q24 tooling context.
