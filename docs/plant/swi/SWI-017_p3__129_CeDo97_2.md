## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q15 / extruder degassing (Q re: vacuum ontgassing):** Confirms the extruder has **two vacuum (degassing) openings** that must be cleaned with a plamuurmes so the screw is visible and free of burnt melt; a blocked vacuum port → poor **ontgassing** (degassing) → quality problems. Ties to the extruder-vacuum-ontgassing doc (067_CeDo70). Directly supports the melt/vacuum-zone context of the temperature-zone question.
- **Heetafslag (hot die-face cutter) detail:** Step 8 confirms the die-face pelletizer ("heetafslag kop") with its own **water pump**; on shutdown the water pump is off, then the heetafslag head is opened and the vacuum lids set aside. Relevant to Q5 (granuleerwater loop) and Q7 (tankje tussen extruders): the heetafslag has a dedicated water pump / pelletizing water loop.
- **Safety (red cross, step 9):** Compactor internal cleanout requires **leather gloves**; compactor stays very hot after stop. Side lamp indicates run/stop state.
- No new numeric setpoints on this page (procedural/cleaning steps).
