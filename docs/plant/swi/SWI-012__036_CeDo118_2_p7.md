## Notes / extracted facts (OPEN QUESTIONS hits)
- **Q30 (power_CC unit):** strong evidence — the compactor's "vermogen" is SET in **KW** on a dial: warm-up at **70 KW**, production setting **140 KW**. So compactor power ("power_CC" = compactor power [unsure: CC meaning]) is expressed in kW, not amps. Ramp rule: 70 KW until 100 °C, then 140 KW; proceed when 105 °C at 140 KW.
- Compactor temperatures at start-up: 100 °C (end of warm-up) → 105 °C (ready at 140 KW). Compactor has a **kijkglas** (sight glass) marking manual fill level.
- Lijn 1 extruder area has a **granuleerkamer/granulaatkamer** with unlock lever — the pelletizer, consistent with a hot-die-face granulation loop (relevant to Q5 granuleerwater loop hardware).
- Control ergonomics: hand-feed selector knob "naast het beeldscherm" on the extruder; positions: hand toevoer / 0 / automaat.
