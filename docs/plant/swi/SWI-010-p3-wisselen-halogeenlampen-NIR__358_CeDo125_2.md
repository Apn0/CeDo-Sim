## Open-question hits — Q28 (TITECH / TOMRA NIR)
- **Lamp calibration lock:** halogen lamp position is **factory/Tomra-calibrated**; the **borgschroef must NOT be loosened** — changing lamp position "reduces the sorting result considerably." Always swap the **complete halogen lamp unit**, Tomra-approved & pre-calibrated only. → Good sim rule: replacing a NIR lamp requires the calibrated unit; wrong/mispositioned lamp degrades sorting quality.
- **Mechanical detail:** heat exchanger held by **6 screws**; no impact tools; connector **nr. 4** is the lamp power plug.
- Complements SWI-009 p6 (`357_CeDo135`) lamp-intensity **>90%** check → when <90%, this SWI-010 is the replacement procedure.
- No throughput/temp/water values.
