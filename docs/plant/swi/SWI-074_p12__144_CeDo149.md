## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q15 / laserfilter internals:** Confirms **TWO discharge augers ("afvoervijzel I" and "afvoervijzel II")** in the LF 2 twin-chamber filter, plus the **schraper** that lifts off the jacking-screw head. In service the scraper and clamp ring cake up with **congealed polymer melt ("overtollige smelt")** — this is exactly the "lump" material the user's laser-filter sim discharges. So the real machine's contaminant discharge is congealed melt scraped off the screen and pushed out by the augers. Reinforces the twin-auger, scraper-on-jacking-screw architecture.
