## Notes / extracted facts (OPEN QUESTIONS hits)
- **Laserfilter lump discharge confirmed in an official SWI:** the **uitdraai schroef** "transporteert de verontreinigingen naar buiten" — this is the contaminant (lump) discharge screw of the rotary disc melt filter; melt pressure is high enough that the screw needs a **borgpen** so it isn't pushed out of its bush. Matches the sim's laser-filter → corrugated hose → lump_cart chain; the screw is the component that pushes lumps out. Step title "schroef (en)" (plural) matches SWI-074's vijzel I + vijzel II.
- **Lijn 1 ALSO has a laserfilter** — direct quote: "Lijn 3 kleine kop, Lijn 1 heeft grote kop" (door-bolt head sizes differ per line). So both lijn 1 and lijn 3 extruders run laserfilters.
- Laserfilter **door is held by 32 bolts**, which must be clean and greased (thread + under head) before reassembly; copper/assembly paste tin visible.
