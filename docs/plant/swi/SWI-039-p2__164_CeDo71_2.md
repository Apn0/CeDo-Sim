## Notes / answers — bunker / sorting-line
- **"Bunkerrol" = a roller inside the line-3 bunker** that gets wrapped with film + wires and must be cleaned with a utility knife + bolt cutters. This is the physical roller at the bunker feeding the sorting line.
- **Failure-mode note (usable for sim):** loose film/wires left in the bunker or on the front of the conveyor cause **blockages elsewhere in the sorting line at start-up** — a concrete cause→effect for jam mechanics. Ties to **Q20** (slow-running causes → remedies): contaminated bunker roller → downstream blockage; remedy = clean roller + clear belt, dispose in waste container.
- LOTO discipline: padlock applied on p1 (rotor/roller lock), removed at final step — same brass-padlock-on-rotary-switch device seen on the Tomra belt (SWI-009 p5).
- Cross-links to SWI-049 (sortline start-up: bunkerdeuren closed, shredders in auto) and the bunker context for **Q16** "Bunker speed 200-800".
