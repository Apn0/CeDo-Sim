## Cycle diagram (as drawn) — ring around center "La1"

Blue ring with 8 yellow dots (stations 1–8) and central recycle arrows labeled **"La1"**. Around the ring each station is annotated with what flows there (clockwise from top):

- **1 (top): water** — Waterbak La1
- **2 (upper-right): water** — Pomp P1
- **3 (right): water/folie** — Doseerschroef
- **4 (lower-right): water/folie** — Frictiewasser
- **5 (bottom): water/folie** — Glijgoot
- **6 (lower-left): water/folie** — Pomp C1
- **7 (left): water/folie — "worden gescheiden van mekaar"** — Frictiescheider M3 (here water & film are **separated from each other**)
- **8 (upper-left): water** — Zandscheider (clean water after sand separation)

**Flow (clockwise):** Waterbak La1 (1) → Pomp P1 (2) → Doseerschroef (3) → Frictiewasser (4) → Glijgoot (5) → Pomp C1 (6) → Frictiescheider M3 (7, water/film separated) → Zandscheider (8, sand removed, water clean) → back to Waterbak La1 (1). Closed recirculating loop.

## Notes / relevance to open questions — MAJOR HITS

- **Q2 (What is block C1 on lijn 3A between Frictiewasser M1/M2 and Frictiescheider M3?) — RESOLVED.** **C1 = "Pomp C1" (Pump C1)** — it is station 6 in the La1 circuit, sitting **between the Frictiewasser/Glijgoot (stations 4–5) and the Frictiescheider M3 (station 7)**. So the "C1" block on lijn 3A between the friction washer and the friction separator M3 is a **pump (Pomp C1)** that moves the water/film slurry from the glijgoot/friction-washer over to the friction separator. Evidence: legend "6- Pomp C1"; ring position 6 is between Frictiewasser(4)/Glijgoot(5) and Frictiescheider M3(7). ✔ (Note the water-circuit slide uses generic "Frictiewasser"; the machine-layout doc splits it into M1/M2 — C1 is the transfer pump after them.)
- **Q11 (LA1/LA2) — CONFIRMED.** La1 = **Water circuit 1**; its tank is **Waterbak La1**. Consistent with La2 slide (113_CeDo9). Letters "La" = water-circuit/tank designation (still not expanded to full words on slide). Evidence: title + "1- Waterbak La 1".
- **Q5 (LA2/P1 return to flotatie tank) — refines.** This slide shows **Pomp P1 is the La1 circuit pump** (station 2), feeding Doseerschroef → Frictiewasser. So P1 belongs to **La1** (not La2). The La1 loop does NOT go to a flotatietank — it uses **Frictiescheider M3 + Zandscheider** for separation. (The flotatietank belongs to the La2 loop, 113_CeDo9.) So "LA2/P1" in the open question conflates two circuits: **P1 = La1 pump, P2 = La2 pump.** Evidence: "2- Pomp P1" here vs "2- Pomp P2" on 113_CeDo9.
- **Machine inventory (wash 3a La1 loop):** Waterbak La1, **Pomp P1**, **Doseerschroef**, **Frictiewasser**, **Glijgoot**, **Pomp C1**, **Frictiescheider M3**, **Zandscheider**. This is the ordered process train of wash line 3a's La1 circuit — very useful for the plant map/SCADA modeling.
- **Zandscheider (sand separator, station 8)** is a newly-named 3a machine — removes sand/grit before water returns clean to the Waterbak.
