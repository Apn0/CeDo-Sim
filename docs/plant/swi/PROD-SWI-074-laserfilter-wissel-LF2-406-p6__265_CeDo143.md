## Values / tools captured verbatim
- **Dopmoeren: 3 stuks** (3 cap nuts) — undone with a **steek/ring sleutel 13 mm** (13 mm combination spanner).
- LOTO: werkschakelaar OFF + **hangslot** (padlock) on the isolator (opposite side of the filter).
- **compactbuis** must be removed to free the **beschermkap**.
- Watch **bekabeling aan scharnierzijde** (cabling on the hinge side) when pulling/swinging the cover.
