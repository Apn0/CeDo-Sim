## Content — 6 labelled close-up photos of screen discs (3 columns × 2 rows)

The page is a matrix of macro photographs of perforated laserfilter screen discs, each labelled with a Dutch defect category. These are the rejection/inspection criteria — each photo shows a failure mode to recognize when deciding on reuse.

| Position | Label (NL, verbatim) | Gloss (EN) | Photo description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Col1, top | **Vullers** | "Fillers" — holes clogged/filled with embedded material | Perforated disc with several holes packed with white/pale plug material (contaminant fillers lodged in the perforations). |
| Col1, bottom | **Mechanische schade** | Mechanical damage | Disc surface with diagonal scoring/scratches and gouges running across the perforation field (mechanical scoring damage). |
| Col2, top | **Corrosie** | Corrosion | Dense perforated mesh with dark corrosion pitting/staining spread across the holes. |
| Col2, bottom | **Minimale schermdikte genegeerd** | Minimum screen thickness ignored/disregarded | Fine mesh screen with a large smeared/worn pale patch where the screen has been ground/worn below minimum thickness (a smeared blob region). |
| Col3, top | **Zand en glas** | Sand and glass | Disc with holes containing embedded shiny white/glassy and sandy particles (abrasive sand & glass contamination lodged in perforations). |
| Col3, bottom | **gaten uitgewassen** | holes washed out / eroded open | Disc with perforations enlarged and eroded (holes "washed out"/widened), yellowish worn rims around each hole. |

## Interpretation for the sim (defect taxonomy)
The six failure modes for used laserfilter screen discs, i.e. reasons a disc fails reuse inspection:
1. **Vullers** — perforations plugged with filler contaminant.
2. **Mechanische schade** — physical scoring/gouging.
3. **Corrosie** — corrosion pitting.
4. **Minimale schermdikte genegeerd** — worn below minimum screen thickness (over-ground).
5. **Zand en glas** — abrasive sand/glass embedded in holes.
6. **gaten uitgewassen** — holes eroded/widened open by wash-out.
