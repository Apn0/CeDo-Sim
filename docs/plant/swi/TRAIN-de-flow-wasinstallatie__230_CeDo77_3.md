## Answers to open questions (HIGH VALUE — this is the master flow)
- **Q2 (block C1 on 3A between Frictiewasser M1/M2 and Frictiescheider M3):** CONFIRMED position — in the flow **Frictiewasser (M1 en M2) → C1 → Frictiescheider M3**. C1 sits exactly between the friction washer (M1/M2) and the friction separator M3. [The diagram labels it only "C1" — still no expansion; it is a numbered stage/component C1 in the wash train, likely a connecting/transport or tank element between wash and separation. Best reading: C1 = a buffer/transport stage.]
- **Q3 (is M11a shared by Mengsilo dosing screw AND infeed screw?):** CONFIRMED — **"Doseerschroef M11a" appears TWICE** in the flow: (a) at the very front as the **infeed dosing screw** after Vuilsnipper silo 3a, and (b) again in the **Mengsilo → Doseerschroef M11a → Verdeelwals (m14)** sub-loop. So the **M11a designation is used for both the infeed dosing screw and the mengsilo dosing screw** — supporting that M11a is shared/reused. (Also a **Doseerschroef M11b** exists in the return row — so M11a and M11b are distinct dosing screws, M11a used in two spots.)
- **Q4 (Rafter):** Not named "Rafter" here; the flotatie-tank block group (Intrekrol → Flotatie tank → Uittrek rol → Ontwater schroef) is the flotation/dewatering assembly — the "rafter" in other SWIs is the chain-scraper/roll unit on the flotation tank of 3b (the intrek/uittrek roll + scraper). This flow shows the 3a equivalent with **intrekrol + uittrekrol + ontwaterschroef** on the flotatie tank.
- **Q7 (tankje tussen extruders / flotatie):** The flotatie tank on this line has an **intrekrol (draw-in roll)** and **uittrekrol (draw-out roll)** plus its own **ontwaterschroef (dewatering screw)**; downstream a second **Frictiescheider M4** and **Mechanische droger M8**.
- **Q8 (does 3B have a bigbag station?):** A **bigbag station** IS present in the flow, branching down from the **Weegschaal (weighing scale)** after the centrifuge, in parallel with the **MS/LS silo buiten**. So product goes weighing → either MS/LS outdoor silo OR bigbag station. (Confirms a bigbag station exists on this wash/extrusion line; whether specifically 3B vs 3A shared is not disambiguated on this general-flow chart.)
- **Q15 (laserfilter / extruder):** Extrusion stage explicitly = **"Extruder laserfilter vacuum"** → **"Kopfilter & heetafslag"** (head/die filter + hot die-face cut). Confirms laserfilter is integral to the extruder with **vacuum degassing**, and pelletizing is **heetafslag (hot cut)** at the die head with a kopfilter.
- **Q5 (water routing / dewatering):** After extrusion, pellets go **Ontwaterzeef en centrifuge → Weegschaal**. The flotatie tank has a dedicated **ontwaterschroef**. Dewatering appears at two points: flotatie-tank dewatering screw (wash side) and ontwaterzeef+centrifuge (extrusion side, before weighing).
- **Q22 / machine numbering (motor map, general line):** 
  - M1, M2 = Frictiewasser (friction washer)
  - M3 = Frictiescheider (friction separator)  [and per doc 229, M3 = ontwaterschroef on La2 circuit — M3 spans the friction-separation/dewatering stage]
  - M4 = Frictiescheider (second)
  - M8 = Mechanische droger (mechanical dryer) + intrek schroef
  - M11a = Doseerschroef (infeed AND mengsilo dosing screw — shared)
  - M11b = Doseerschroef (return/mengsilo)
  - m14 = Verdeelwals (distribution roller)
  - V1 (rondmengen), V2, V2a, V3, V4 = Ventilators/fans; V3 after Cycloon on thermal-dryer branch
- **Dryer architecture:** TWO dryers — **Mechanische droger M8** (mechanical) and **Thermische droger** (thermal, heated, with its own verdeelwals + cyclone → V3). Air handling via Cycloon & windzifter, ringleiding, multiple ventilators.
- **Termination:** Product ends at **MS / LS silo buiten** (outdoor MS/LS silos — relevant to Q27, the 10 outdoor silos: MS and LS product grades stored outside) or **bigbag station**.
