## Values / parts / tools captured verbatim
- **brakerplate** (breaker plate) — cleaned each change.
- **doorvoer opening in de deur** (through-passage opening in the door).
- Cleaning tools: **plamuurmes en buis** (putty knife + tube), **speciale staalborstel** (special steel brush).
- **afvoervijzel II** (discharge screw/auger II) — re-fitted last; cleaning the passage first makes re-fitting easier. (This is the contaminant/lump discharge auger — matches the user's laser_filter note about the corrugated-bottom discharge and lump handling.)
- **schraper** (scraper) with **aandrijfopening** (drive opening).
- **koperen ring** = single-use copper/bronze wear ring in the scraper drive opening — **discard and replace every change** ("kan niet meer gebruikt worden").
- Housekeeping rule (yellow note): clean attachments immediately after use.
