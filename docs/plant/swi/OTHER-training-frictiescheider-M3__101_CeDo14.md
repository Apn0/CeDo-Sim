## Content (verbatim NL + EN gloss)

**Diagram:** An inclined tubular friction separator drawn diagonally (low-left rising to high-right). An **E-Motor** at the top-right end drives the internal rotor via a **V-snaar overbrenging** (V-belt transmission). The tube contains a **rotor with paddels (paddles)** and a **zeef (screen/sieve)** along its underside (full length). Black chunks (film flakes + dirt) are shown moving through. Flow arrows: **"Water"** flows down/left, **"Folie" (film)** is conveyed up/right.

**Callout labels (as printed):**
- **"Vuil water en folie vanuit pomp C1"** (dirty water and film coming IN from pump C1) — arrow entering the LOW (bottom-left) end.
- **"V-snaar overbrenging"** (V-belt transmission) — at the motor/top end.
- **"Paddels"** (paddles) — internal rotor blades.
- **"Product richting flotatietank"** (product toward the flotation tank) — exiting the HIGH (top-right) end, film conveyed onward.
- **"Zeef"** (screen/sieve) — the perforated underside screen along the rotor length.
- **"Waterafvoer + vuil"** (water discharge + dirt) — drains out the bottom/low end through the screen.

**Body text:**
> "Het water en vuil wordt in deze frictiescheiders gescheiden van de folie. De elektromotor drijft de rotor aan. Aan de onderzijde van de rotor over de gehele lengte zit een zeef. Het materiaal wordt van beneden naar boven getransporteerd. Tijdens dit transporteren door de rotor wordt het water en vuil door het zeef afgevoerd."

The material is transported from bottom to top. During this transport by the rotor, the water and dirt are discharged through the screen."

## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q2 (block C1 on lijn 3A between Frictiewasser M1/M2 and Frictiescheider M3):** **ANSWERED.** **C1 is a pump ("pomp C1")** that delivers the **dirty water + film ("vuil water en folie") into the frictiescheider M3.** So on lijn 3A, after the Frictiewasser (friction washers M1/M2), pump **C1 lifts/transfers the water+film slurry into the friction separator M3.** C1 = transfer/feed pump, not a separate separation stage.
- **Q6 (sieve/zeefbocht):** M3 has a **full-length underside screen (zeef)** through which **water + dirt are drained** while the rotor+paddles convey the film upward. This is the dewatering screen of the friction separator (the "pomp zeefbocht" likely refers to a screen elbow on the pump/drain circuit of exactly this kind of unit — screening out dirt from the water discharge). Water leaves at the low end ("waterafvoer+vuil"); clean(er) film exits the high end.
- **Q5 (water routing — LA2/P1 return to flotatie tank):** Confirms the **product (film) from M3 goes "richting flotatietank" (toward the flotation tank)** — the friction separator feeds the flotation tank downstream. Water+dirt drains off separately.
- **Machine identity (M3):** **Frictiescheider (friction separator)** = an inclined screw/paddle friction dewatering separator, **E-motor + V-belt driven rotor with paddles, full-length bottom screen.** Transports film from bottom to top, drains water/dirt through the screen, discharges film to the flotation tank. (Distinct from M1/M2 Frictiewasser = friction washers upstream.)
