## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q9 (line capacities / throughput) & general product spec:** This gives **batch/lot tonnages per product grade**, tied to the sister CeDo plants that consume the recycled pellets:
  - L1 → 28 ton (ships to **Telford** 28 t, or **Wrocław** 25 t)
  - L3a → 28 ton (ships to **Telford** 28 t)
  - L3b → 25 ton (ships to **Wrocław** 25 t)
  - "Telford" = CeDo UK plant; "Wroclow" = Wrocław (CeDo Poland plant). The 28 t vs 25 t split appears to be the standard full-container/lot size per destination.
- Ties to 121_CeDo33 (laserfilter grades L1/L3a/L3b) — same L1/L3a/L3b product-grade naming, here mapped to lot tonnage and destination plant rather than to production lines.
