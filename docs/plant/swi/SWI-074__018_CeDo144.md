## Cross-references / open-question notes
- Names internal laserfilter parts: **uitzetschroef/uittrekschroef** (jacking screw), **afvoervijzel II** (discharge screw II — implies an afvoervijzel I as well). The afvoervijzel is what discharges the contaminant "lumps" — relevant to the lump_cart discharge modelled in the sim.
- Machine ID "LF 2 - 406" strongly suggests **EREMA Laserfilter 2/406** (Q4-adjacent; also fits with EREMA INTAREMA brochures nrs 352/353 present in the same folder).
