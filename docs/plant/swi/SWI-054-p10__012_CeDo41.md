## Notes / open-question hits
- New term: **binnenste afvoervijzel** (inner discharge auger) — synonym/inner part of the uitdraaischroef assembly; extracted horizontally with a threaded **spindel met oog** + **kettingtakel** (chain hoist) strapped to a building column.
- Rigging safety verbatim: strop must not pinch anything ("let op dat de strob niets afknelt"); auger may need real force to break loose though it "in principe los" sits.
- Sim-relevant: the laserfilter change occupies the walkway (spindle sticks out ~2 m), needs a chain hoist anchor point — layout constraint for the lijn 3 extruder platform model.
