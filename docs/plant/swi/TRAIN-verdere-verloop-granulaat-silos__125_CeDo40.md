## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q27 (the 10 outdoor silos — contents/function):** Strong support. The post-extruder flow uses outdoor silos in two roles: **Voorraad silo** (storage/buffer of freshly extruded granulate) and **Meng / bulk silo** (mixing / bulk homogenization silo) before **Transport** (dispatch). So the outdoor silo farm holds finished recycled pellets — some as per-line storage buffers, some as mixing/bulk blend silos feeding truck loadout. Conveying is pneumatic (**air blower + compressor + leidingwerk**).
- **Q5 (water/material routing) & Q19 (stortgewicht):** The **weegschaal** step sits directly after the extruder and before pneumatic conveying — this is where granulate batches are weighed (relevant to the weegschaal/bulk-weight questions).
- **Q30 (units):** No numeric units on this slide, but confirms a **Weegschaal** (weighing scale) is a discrete station after the extruder — the same weegschaal referenced elsewhere (TRAIN-de-weegschaal-p6c).
- Chain confirms sim material path: extruder pellets → weigh → pneumatic convey (blower) → voorraadsilo → mengsilo/bulksilo → truck transport.
