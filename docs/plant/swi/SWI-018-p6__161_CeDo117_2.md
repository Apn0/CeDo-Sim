## Notes / answers — line-1 material flow
- **NEW machine name: "MAS drogers" (MAS dryers)** feed the **extruder silo** on line 1. MAS = an OEM (likely **MAS Maschinen- und Anlagenbau Schulz** — twin-conical-screw extruder/dryer maker). Film is dried by the MAS dryers, buffered in the extruder silo, then the **compactor** is filled from there → into the extruder. This nails a piece of the line-1 upstream flow: **MAS dryers → extruder silo → compactor → extruder**.
- **Water tank (waterbak)** has a pump with a suction-intake filter (must be clean) and a fill shut-off valve — this is process/cooling water on line 1. Relevant to **Q5** (water routing) and **Q6** (sieve/filter screening — here it's the pump-suction filter, a different element than "pomp zeefbocht").
- Feed-start trigger for line 1: **fill the compactor only once film is seen entering the extruder silo from the MAS dryers** — a concrete start-up sequencing rule usable in the sim.
- Confirms line 1 has: German bagging weighing scale (p5), 6-knife heetafslag pelletiser (p5), MAS dryers, extruder silo, compactor, extruder. Directly informs **Q24** (feed is dried film crumb by this stage, pre-compactor).
