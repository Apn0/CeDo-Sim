## Verbatim content

Header: **De Compactorband**

Subheading: **-1-**

Body text (verbatim, Dutch):
> "Het toevoegen van materiaal naar de compactor, gebeurd door de toevoerband
> Band gaat draaien als de compactor materiaal vraagt en stopt als de compactor zijn ingestelde waarde heeft bereikt"

English gloss: "Adding material to the compactor is done by the feed belt (toevoerband). The belt starts turning when the compactor requests material and stops when the compactor has reached its set value (ingestelde waarde)."

## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q24 / feed-density context (line 1 material flow):** Confirms the compactor on **line 1** is fed by a demand-driven **toevoerband** (feed conveyor): belt runs on compactor demand and halts at the compactor's setpoint ("ingestelde waarde"). This is the pre-compactor film feed step (film → compactor → crumb), relevant to the pre-compactor film density vs post-compactor crumb density distinction.
