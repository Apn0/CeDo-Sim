## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q4 (Rafter 3b):** Confirms "rafter" is a machine/spray section on **lijn 3b** with its own anti-foam spray installation ("rafter 3b een beetje open"). It sits in the wash-line group alongside "was 3a" and "was 3b". (Make/type not given here.)
- **Q11 (LA1/LA2):** "LA1 was 3a" is named — **LA1 is associated with the wash section of line 3a** (a wash unit/tank designation). Full expansion of LA still not spelled out, but LA1 is a physical wash unit on 3a.
- **Anti-foam setpoints:** Lijn 3a anti-foam on **manual 60%**, lijn 3b anti-foam on **automatic 40%**. Two yellow diaphragm dosing pumps.
- **Startup logic:** Wash installation started in AUTOMAAT (automatic); settings pulled back then ramped up gradually; dosings on and line filled so the **extruder starts on time** (line 3 = wash+sort feeding an extruder).
