## Values / settings
- 2 sloten (locks) per sorteermachine, on 2 werkschakelaars (sorteerband + rol).

## Cross-references / open-question notes (Q28)
- **There are TWO sorting machines: "Titech 1 of 2"** — each with its own sorteerband (sorting/acceleration belt) and rol (roller), each LOTOTO'd separately.
- Components named: **luchtbalk** (air/valve nozzle bar, opened via sleutelschakelaar for cleaning — this is the compressed-air ejection bar), **rol**, **sorteerband**, operator **beeldscherm** with storingen-reset.
- The room is called "Tomra & Titech sorteer ruimte" — both brand names used together (TITECH was acquired by TOMRA), consistent with "Werking Titech tomra" doc.
- No sorting-program/eject-target info on this page (that would be pages 1/3 or the "Werking Titech tomra" doc).
