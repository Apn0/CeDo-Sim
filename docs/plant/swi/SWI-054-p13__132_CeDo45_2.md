## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q15 / laserfilter spec:** The **Siebscheibe (screen disc) is 130–150 µm** — matches the "L1 130-150 Mµ" grade from 121_CeDo33. So the laserfilter disc has a German-labelled OEM spec **"Siebscheibe 130 – 150µm"**, Mat.Nr 10121667_130. Confirms the OEM is German (Siebscheibe = screen disc; consistent with a Britas/German laser filter). Real part number captured: **Mat.Nr. 10121667_130**.
- **Q33 (does a filled-in list exist):** YES-adjacent — step 50 references **"de daarvoor bestemde lijst die bij de weegschaal hangt"** and the photo shows a **filled-in paper log grid** hanging by the weegschaal where each installed filter number is recorded. This is the real hand-written filter-registration list (a filled-in operational log exists at the weegschaal).
- **Q3 / screws:** Confirms the laserfilter has an **uitdraaischroef / afvoervijzel** (discharge screw) with a **borgpinnetje** (locking pin) that must be checked before reassembly.
- **Weegschaal location:** The filter-registration list "hangs by the weegschaal" — places the weegschaal near the extruder/laserfilter (consistent with 125_CeDo40 flow: extruder → weegschaal).
