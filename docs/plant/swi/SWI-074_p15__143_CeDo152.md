## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q15 / laserfilter:** More construction detail — the **laserfilterhuis (housing)** has a bolt-flange (many holes), a sealing **flens (flange)** cleaned all round, and a **doorgang/sponning (passage in a rebate groove)** at the bottom that is the discharge port for filtered contaminant. Cleaning uses a dedicated wire brush + angle-grinder cup brush; metal splinters must be cleared from the port last. Confirms fine-mesh screen + perforated die-plate support construction (visible in photos). Consistent with the twin-chamber melt filter documented on p11/p19.
