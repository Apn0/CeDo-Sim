## Open-question hits

- **Q (float/sink separation physics) — STRONG:** the flotation tank is a **density separator**. Target film (LDPE/PP, SG < 1.0) floats and is carried forward by the **schoepen** to the **uitdraaischroef**; contaminants with **SG > 1.0** (e.g. sand, glass, metal, PET, PVC, other heavy plastics, dirt) **sink** and are dragged out by the **ketting schraper** to a **waste container**. This is the core sink/float wash-line mechanic — directly modellable: incoming stream splits by density at the flotation tank; heavies → reject/waste, lights → onward to drying/extrusion.
- **Q1 / plant labels — tank ID confirmed:** the flotation tank's plant tag is **"LA3"** (on the yellow machine label). Feed device upstream is friction screw **"M3"**. These are concrete plant-floor component IDs usable as in-game labels for the wash line.
- **Wash-line topology:** feed (M3 friction screw) → inlaat paddel → flotatietank (LA3) → [floats: schoepen → uitdraaischroef → onward] / [sinks: ketting schraper → afval container]. Establishes the order of components around the flotation stage.
- No numeric throughput/temperature/flow values on this sheet (qualitative process poster).
