## Lead-in text (verbatim, Dutch)

> "Het vers kanaalwater wordt toegevoegd aan bestaande circuits"

**English gloss:** "The fresh canal water is added to existing circuits" (fresh water is make-up to the other water circuits, e.g. the blue tank / wash installations).

## Legend (verbatim, Dutch) — the 4 stations

> 1- hoofd leiding vanuit kelder      ("main pipe from the cellar/basement")
> 2- blauwe tank                       ("blue tank")
> 3- oa koeling op hydrauliek units    ("among others, cooling on hydraulic units")
> 4- naar wasinstallaties              ("to the wash installations")

## Cycle diagram (as drawn)

Blue ring with 4 yellow dots (stations 1–4) and two central recycle arrows. Positions: **1 top, 2 right, 3 bottom, 4 left.** Flow proceeds around the ring 1 → 2 → 3 → 4:
- **1 (top): hoofd leiding vanuit kelder** — fresh canal water enters via the main pipe from the cellar.
- **2 (right): blauwe tank** — feeds/tops up the blue tank.
- **3 (bottom): oa koeling op hydrauliek units** — also used for cooling on the hydraulic units.
- **4 (left): naar wasinstallaties** — and sent onward to the wash installations.

## Notes / relevance to open questions — HITS

- **Q5 (fresh water routing) — DIRECT.** Fresh canal water path: **main pipe from cellar → blue tank → (cooling on hydraulic units) → wash installations.** Fresh water is the top-up/make-up that flows into the blue tank and out to wash + hydraulic-unit cooling. Confirms the "Kanaalwater → Blauwe tank" arrow seen in 111_CeDo10. Evidence: legend 1–4 above.
- **Q10 (blue tank).** Confirms the **blue tank is fed by fresh canal water via the main pipe from the cellar** (station 1→2). Still no volume/pump specs.
- **Q12 / cooling.** Fresh canal water provides **cooling on the hydraulic units** ("oa koeling op hydrauliek units") — relevant to extruder/hydraulics cooling water routing.
