## 1. Doel Document (Purpose)
"Het standaard reinigings programma (3 programma's per NIR in juiste **volgorde**: **1: cleaning, 2: automatic, 3: manual**) laten lopen van de Titech's en Tomra's op **woensdag morgen om uiterlijk 08:00 uur** tijdens regulier onderhoud en vervolgens de defecte ventielen vervangen (**vanaf 10-20 stuks, zie detail beschrijving punt 26**). (resultaat melden bij TD zoals beschreven voor 10:00 uur zodat deze voor opstart defecten kunnen vervangen)."
- Translation: Run the standard cleaning program (3 programs per NIR in the correct order: 1: cleaning, 2: automatic, 3: manual) on the Titechs and Tomras on **Wednesday morning by 08:00** during regular maintenance, then replace defective valves (**from 10–20 units, see detail description point 26**). Report the result to TD (Technische Dienst / maintenance) before 10:00 so defects can be replaced before startup.

## Answers to open questions
- **Q28 (TITECH programs/ejected/EM sensor/belt width):** PARTIAL — **TITECH/Tomra cleaning uses 3 programs per NIR in order: (1) cleaning, (2) automatic, (3) manual.** Run weekly, Wednesday morning by 08:00. - Confirms the sorter has an **air bar (luchtbalk)** of ejection valves fed by a **dehumidification system** (moisture/oil separator with reservoir) to keep the compressed air dry — critical for valve life and sort quality.
