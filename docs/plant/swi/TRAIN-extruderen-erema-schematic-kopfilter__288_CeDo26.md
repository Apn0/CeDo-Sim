# TRAIN — Extruderen: EREMA line schematic + kopfilter (flipchart step -5-)

- **Source scan:** 288_CeDo26.pdf (1 page, photo of training flipchart)
- **Type:** Cedo Kunstofrecycling training flipchart, **2024**, Lean Practitioner badge.
- **Title:** **Extruderen** ("Extruding")
- **Slide marker:** **-5-** (fifth step of the extrusion training deck)

## Schematic (EREMA single-screw recycling extruder line, isometric drawing)
Numbered callouts 1–6 along the line (left→right):
| # | location on drawing | interpretation |
|---|---------------------|----------------|
| 1 | roll/reel + upward conveyor at far left | Material infeed — feed roll/bunker onto inclined conveyor into the cutter-compactor |
| 2 | tall orange hopper labeled "**counter current**" feeding down into the EREMA barrel | **Counter Current** cutter-compactor / feed unit (EREMA "Counter Current" technology — material fed against screw direction); orange = hot melt entering barrel |
| 3 | along the EREMA screw barrel (red screw shown) | Extruder screw (single screw) — transport + melt |
| 4 | mid-barrel, two up-arrows | Degassing / vacuum vent zone (ontgassing, up-arrows = gas extraction) |
| 5 | red butterfly/valve shape near end | **Kopfilter** (head/melt filter) housing — the melt filter with screens |
| 6 | die/pelletizer at far right | Die plate / pelletizer outlet |

Barrel bears the **EREMA** logo (confirms EREMA extruder).

## Body text (verbatim)
"**-5-** Het opgesmolten materiaal (smelt) gaat vervolgens nog door een kopfilter heen waarin filters zitten die de verontreinigingen tegenhouden."
("-5- The molten material (melt) then passes through a head filter (kopfilter) which contains filters that hold back the contaminants.")

## Answers found
- **Extruder process confirmation:** EREMA single-screw line with **Counter Current** feed (callout 2), degas/vacuum zone (4), **kopfilter/melt filter** (5) before the die (6). The kopfilter holds back contaminants — this is the filter stage upstream of / alongside the laserfilter.
- **Q15 (temp zones before/after laserfilter):** context — melt path is barrel (transport/melt/degas) → kopfilter → die. The laserfilter (rotary disc, Britas-style) is the automated self-cleaning version of this kopfilter stage; temp zones straddle the barrel then the filter. (Numeric zone values not on this slide.)
- **"Counter Current"** = EREMA's counter-current feed unit (Gegenstrom) — relevant plant terminology for the CeDo Simulator extruder model.
