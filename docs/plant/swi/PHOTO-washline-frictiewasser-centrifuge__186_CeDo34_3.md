## Photo description (one-line-per-object, exhaustive)
Night/indoor plant floor, heavy steam/mist plume rising from the machines under overhead LED lights and grey process piping.

- **Overhead:** cable-tray ladder rack, round ducting, and a vertical insulated stack/chimney on the far left; catwalk railing top-right.
