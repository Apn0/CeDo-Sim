## Notes / extracted facts (OPEN QUESTIONS hits)
- **Q28 (TOMRA):** the title is plural — "Ventielen **Tomra's**" — and the step-11 photos show two different machine number plates, **3132** and **3122**, on blue housings. Strong evidence the plant had (at least) **two Tomra sorting machines**, with plant asset numbers 3122 and 3132.
- TOMRA ejection hardware named: **luchtbalk** (air/valve bar) with **kolven** (valve pistons), a **hoofdafsluiter** (main air shut-off, callout 1) and an **ontvochtiging** (moisture-drain/dehumidifier valve, callout 4) — i.e., compressed-air ejection with a moisture-management circuit that gets drained during cleaning and closed again afterwards.
- Maintenance sequence on this page = re-commissioning end of the valve-clean: reopen main air, leak-check pistons, close moisture drain, then secure installation for the next job.
