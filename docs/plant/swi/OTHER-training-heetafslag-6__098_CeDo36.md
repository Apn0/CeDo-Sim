EN: "The melt is pressed through a die (matrijs). The hot-cut cutter (heetafslag) then cuts the melt to the correct length. Through the water that circulates continuously, the re-granulate is carried off to the dewatering sieve (ontwaterzeef)."

## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q5 (granuleerwater loop):** Confirmed — **continuously circulating water (granuleerwater)** at the die face quenches and **conveys the freshly cut pellets (re-granulaat) to the ontwaterzeef (dewatering sieve).** So the crumb/pellet path is: **extruder melt → matrijs (die) → heetafslag (hot-cut cutter) → granuleerwater carries pellets → ontwaterzeef → centrifuge → weegschaal → silo.** This is a closed granulation-water loop.
- **Q15 (extruder temp zones / laserfilter):** This slide sits downstream of the laserfilter/screen-changer — the melt goes through the **matrijs (die plate)** at the very end. The die-face pelletizer is a **hot-cut / die-face system** (not strand pelletizing). The rotating multi-blade cutter head shears strands to pellet length directly at the die.
- Machine identity: a **hot die-face (heetafslag) pelletizer** — the die plate has an annular ring of pellet holes; cutter is a spinning spider blade. Water-ring / underwater-adjacent style granulation.
