## Product positioning (verbatim key claims)
- "The patented extruder system INTAREMA® TVEplus® sets new standards in the recycling of materials that are difficult to process such as heavily printed films and/or very moist materials."
- "The proven basic principle of TVEplus® technology: **melt filtration takes place upstream of extruder degassing.**"
- Target feedstocks pictured: Washed PE film flakes · Heavily printed PE, PP film · PE film with paper contamination · Metallised BOPP film. **(Q25 relevance: BOPP — metallised BOPP film is an explicit TVEplus feedstock.)**

## Three headline features
1. **Counter Current technology®** — improved material intake → constant high output over broader temperature range; higher flexibility/reliability with varied materials; increased throughput at same plant size. Material inside the Preconditioning Unit rotates OPPOSITE to the extruder screw; relative speed in intake zone rises so the extruder edge "cuts up" the plastic → more material in less time, can run at LOWER temperatures at high throughput.
2. **Smart Start®** — ergonomic touchscreen, high automation, recipe management (saved processing parameters loaded at push of a button).
3. **ecoSAVE®** — lower specific energy (new direct drive for extruder screw), energy display on panel, reduced CO₂. **Reduces energy consumption by up to 12%.**

## Process flow "How it works" (numbered 1–8, verbatim mapping)
1. **Feeding** — automatic, per customer requirements.
2. **Preconditioning Unit (PCU)** — patented; material is cut, mixed, heated, dried, pre-compacted and buffered. Pictogram verbs (verbatim): **cuts · homogenises · heats · dries · compacts · buffers · doses**. Tangentially connected extruder is filled continuously with hot pre-compacted material; Counter Current gives optimised intake over extended temperature range.
3. **Extruder screw** — material plasticised and degassed in reverse.
4. **Filter** — at end of plasticising zone melt is directed out, cleaned in the fully automatic self-cleaning filter (EREMA Laserfilter), returned to extruder.
5. **Final homogenisation** of the melt — after the melt filter.
6. **Degassing zone** — filtered + homogenised material is degassed.
7. **Discharge zone** — conveys melt onward.
8. **Tool** (e.g. pelletiser) — melt delivered at extremely low pressure.

## Optimised triple degassing (verbatim)
- Initial degassing in the PCU via preheating + predrying.
- Optimum screw design enables **reverse degassing** in the PCU, relieving the extruder degassing zone.
- Gas inclusions removed in extruder degassing zone.
- Only thoroughly melted, filtered and homogenised material can pass the degassing zone.

## Optional PCU add-on technologies
- **Air Flush Module** (patented) — increases drying performance + output, lower energy, extends service life. (cross-ref existing `EREMA-air-flush-module__175_CeDo58.md`)
- **Double Disc (DD) technology** (patented) — materials with up to **12% residual moisture** processed at consistently high output.
- Optimised large EREMA Preconditioning Unit.

## Filtration test note (verbatim)
- Comparison test: washed post-consumer films (film sample with 100% recycled pellets), filtered with the **EREMA Laserfilter (110 µm)** — confirms increased filtration performance thanks to minimal shearing upstream of the melt filter.

## Open-question hits
- **Q9 (line throughput kg/h) — STRONG:** This is the reference table. CeDo runs LDPE film. For LDPE/LLDPE film: 1310 = 700–850, 1512 = 950–1200, 1714 = 1250–1550 kg/h. The **1200 kg/h budget (Q21)** aligns with an **INTAREMA 1512 TVEplus** at its LDPE-film MAX (950–1200). So the "1200 kg/hr budget" line is consistent with a 1512 TVEplus running LDPE film at top end — likely the 3C/lijn-6 class machine. (Model number seen elsewhere in HMI docs, e.g. "INTAREMA 1310 TVEplus" label appears in a page-4/5 render.)
- **Q15 (temp zones / why zone3=175):** Brochure confirms TVEplus runs melt filtration UPSTREAM of degassing and enables running at LOWER temperatures via Counter Current. Supports the idea that a low zone (175°C) sits in the intake/pre-filter region for reduced shearing before the Laserfilter (shearing minimised upstream of filter). Not a direct zone map, but explains the low-temp design intent.
- **Q12 (EOP), Q11 (LA1/LA2), Q1 (ZSS):** not addressed (vendor brochure, no CeDo plant labels).
- **Q25 (BOPP on 3A/3B):** confirmed metallised BOPP film is a listed TVEplus feedstock (does not localise to a line but confirms the machine can process BOPP).
- Laserfilter mesh reference: **110 µm** (test filtration value).
