## Content (verbatim NL + EN gloss)

**Illustration:** A stylized isometric of the **EREMA** extrusion line (brand "EREMA" printed on the barrel housing). It features EREMA's **Counter Current** technology (orange "counter current" logo/badge on the cutter-compactor cylinder). Numbered stations:
1. **①** A roll/reel feeding the **inclined infeed conveyor** (blue chevron belt) that carries film flakes up.
2. **②** The **cutter-compactor / Counter Current preconditioning unit** (orange, "counter current" badge) — film drops in and is cut/compacted/preheated; feeds the screw tangentially.
3. **③** The **extruder screw / barrel** (long red-hot melt channel drawn inside the EREMA barrel).
4. **④** A station with **upward arrows** mid-barrel = the **vacuum degassing port** (see body text).
5. **⑤** The **melt filter / screen changer** near the barrel end (drawn as a valve/filter body) — i.e. the laserfilter/screen-changer position.
6. **⑥** The **die / discharge** at the far right (melt exits to pelletizing).

## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Machine make/type (major):** The **extruder is an EREMA line with Counter Current technology** — an EREMA cutter/compactor + single-screw extruder (the plant's LDPE extrusion line). The centrifuge (slide 6b) is also EREMA, so the wash-to-pellet back end is largely **EREMA equipment**.
- **Q15 (extruder zones / vacuum / filter):** Confirms the on-machine order: **infeed conveyor → cutter-compactor (Counter Current, station 2) → screw (3) → vacuum degassing port (4) → melt filter/screen-changer = laserfilter (5) → die (6).** Matches slide 100 (extruderschroef) which places the laserfilter mid-screw with a vacuum/meng zone; here the vacuum degassing is explicitly a suction port removing screw gases before/around the melt filter. The **vacuum zone extracts volatiles ("ontstane gassen") from the melt**, which is why degassing sits between mixing and the filter.
- **Counter Current:** EREMA's reverse-rotation preconditioning (drum rotates against material flow) improves drying/energy transfer of the LDPE film flakes before the screw — relevant to modeling the compactor→extruder feed and throughput.
