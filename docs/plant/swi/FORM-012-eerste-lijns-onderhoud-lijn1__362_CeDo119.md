## Open-question hits — MAJOR (plant topology / line 1 process order)

- **FULL LINE-1 MACHINE SEQUENCE (process order, verbatim section names) — the definitive topology answer:**
  1. **Banden** (conveyor belts, with **metaaldetectie band** = metal-detection belt)
  2. **Shredder** (zeefkorf = screen basket; rotor/stator knives)
  3. **Trommel** (rotating drum screen; stone removal)
  4. **Fricki's / Intensiefwassers** (friction washers / intensive washers; heavy-parts outlet)
  5. **Maalmolen 1 / MM1** (granulator/mill; rotor + zeef)
  6. **Ventilatoren 10a/10b** (fans, impeller/waaierhuis)
  7. **Cyclonen** (cyclones)
  8. **Flotatie tank** (flotation/sink-float tank with scraper — matches `360_CeDo83` LA3)
  9. **Fricki links rechts** (fricki left/right; screens + bottom water drain)
  10. **Kufferath 1 en 2** (Kufferath screens; bottom water drain)
  11. **MAS drogers** (MAS mechanical dryers; screens checked for holes)
  12. **Extruder** section: opvoerband → **Compactor** → extruder silo → **Laserfilter** → **Vacuum pot / vacuum openings** (degassing) → **Heetafslag ring** (hot-cut pelletiser + knives) → **Waterbak** (water bath) → **Waterfilters** → **Centrifuge** (pellet dewatering) → **Weegschaal + cycloon** (weigh-scale + cyclone).
  → This is directly modellable as the line-1 flow graph for the sim. Wash side (belts→shredder→trommel→fricki→maalmolen→fans→cyclones→flotation→fricki→Kufferath→MAS dryers) then extrusion side (compactor→extruder→laserfilter→vacuum degas→hot-cut→water bath→filters→centrifuge→weigh/cyclone).

- **Laserfilter change rule — CONCRETE NUMERIC (STRONG):** "**Laserfilter wisselen indien eind druk voor stop boven de 180 bar**" = **change the Laserfilter when the end pressure before stop exceeds 180 bar.** This is a hard maintenance threshold — usable as a sim degradation/maintenance trigger (melt-filter pressure climbs with contamination; >180 bar forces a filter change). Complements the live-value HMI melt-pressure readings in `354_CeDo8_3` (286 bar / 113 bar shown) and the throughput tables in `352`.

- **Fans must NOT be used to blow out granulator debris:** "NIET via ventilatoren weg blazen i.v.m. aankoeking troep" — because it causes caking/fouling. A realistic operational constraint / wrong-action penalty.

- **Compactor detail:** "Filter water inspuit compactor reinigen" confirms the compactor has a **water-injection** feature (matches `EREMA-compactor-waterinjectie__177_CeDo60.md`), and melt can cake on the compactor side/rim.

- **Metal detection on belts (Q28-adjacent):** line 1 has a **metaaldetectie band** (metal-detection conveyor) as an early stage — plant-level metal rejection distinct from the TITECH NIR and the in-compactor shock sensor (`356_CeDo61`). Three-layer metal-rejection story: (1) metal-detection belt up front, (2) TITECH/TOMRA NIR sort, (3) compactor shock sensor (PCK) last-ditch.

- **Flotation tank cross-confirm:** "Flotatie tank … Schraper op hand laten draaien" + "Aflaten flotatie tank in overleg met leidinggevende" confirms the flotation tank has a **scraper** (ketting schraper of `360_CeDo83`) and is **drained only with supervisor approval** — a good gated action in the sim.

- **Pelletising tail confirmed:** heetafslag (hot-cut) ring with replaceable knives → water bath → water filters → centrifuge (dewatering) → weigh-scale + cyclone. This is the strand/pellet finishing sequence for the extruder.
