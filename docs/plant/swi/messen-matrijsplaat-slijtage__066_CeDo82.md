## Machine facts extracted

- **Pelletiser hot-cut = rotating knives (messen) running against a die/matrix plate (matrijsplaat).** If the knives are set unevenly (resting on one side, leaving a "gap" on the other), the die plate wears preferentially on that side.
- This is the maintenance rationale behind FORM-018's "Messen wisselen heetafslag beide extruders" (change hot-cut knives on both extruders) — even knife contact / correct gap is required to avoid die-plate damage.
- Reinforces the pelletiser terminology: **heetafslag** = hot-cut; **messen** = knives; **matrijsplaat** = die/matrix plate.
