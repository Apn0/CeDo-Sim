## Notes / answers
- Confirms the **Nordson oven** cleaning line has **two vacuum pots** with removable trays; residue is **chemical waste** (blue drums / chemische afval container). This is the **external/burn-off cleaning station** where fouled melt-filter parts (spanring, laserfilter discs, breaker plates — cf. SWI-054) are pyrolysed and residue collected.
- Ties into Q15/Q24 tooling-maintenance context: the Nordson oven is how carbonised polymer is removed from filter/die hardware.
