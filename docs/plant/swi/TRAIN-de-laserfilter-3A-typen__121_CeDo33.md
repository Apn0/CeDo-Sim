## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q15 (laserfilter context):** Confirms the laserfilter on line **3A** is a fine melt filter; three interchangeable laser-drilled screen discs are used, graded by hole size (micron): L1 = 130-150 µm (finest), L3a = 150-180 µm, L3b = 180-210 µm (coarsest). Naming L1/L3a/L3b appears to map to product/line grades rather than lines per se. Verbatim: "L1 130-150 Mµ / L3a 150-180 Mµ / L3b 180-210 Mµ".
- No make/type printed on this slide, but the machine head photo matches a rotary laser filter (consistent with a Britas-style / laser-drilled disc filter).
