> Natriumhypochloriet (chloor) word gedoseerd toegevoegd
>
> Ter bestrijding van bacteriën  schimmels en virussen (legionella) en voorkomen van algengroei in de koel en proceswater in open doorstromende en recirculerende installaties

**English gloss:** "Sodium hypochlorite (chlorine) is added in dosed amounts. To combat bacteria, fungi and viruses (legionella) and to prevent algae growth in the **cooling and process water** in open once-through and recirculating installations."

Photograph of a **black IBC (1000 L intermediate bulk container)** in a galvanized-steel cage on a pallet, with a red screw cap on top and a teal/green discharge tap/valve at the bottom-front. ## Pictogram meaning

- **GHS05 (corrosive):** the chemical is corrosive to metals and causes skin/eye burns — handle with PPE; relevant to safe-dosing procedures.

## Notes / relevance to open questions — CONTEXT

- **Q5 / water treatment.** Adds detail to the water-circuit picture: **sodium hypochlorite (chlorine) is dosed into the cooling and process water** (open once-through + recirculating loops — i.e. the kanaalwater/blue-tank/EOP circuits) to kill bacteria/fungi/viruses (legionella) and prevent algae.
