## Notes / open-question hits (RICH — this form answers many)
- **Q13 (FORM-008 row 22 duplicate "Compactor 3a rand afzuiging" — should it be 3B?):** CONFIRMED as a **typo in the form**. Rows 21 AND 22 both read "Compactor **3a** rand afzuiging EN reiniging". Row 21 pairs with the 3a compactor (row 19); row 22 is intended to be the **3b** counterpart (pairs with row 20 Compactor 3b temperatuur). The "3a" in row 22 should read **"3b"**. Both targets = 55%.
- **Q14 (FORM-008 row 16 full text "Net overlopen kleine LA 1"):** CONFIRMED full text = **"Nét overlopen kleine LA I"** (just barely overflow the small LA I), parameter "Flotatie tank 3b niveau", target **90-92 CM** (yellow-highlighted). So LA I / "LA 1" is a component/weir at the flotatie tank 3b that should be kept just at overflow. (See Q11.)
- **Q11 (LA1/LA2 meaning):** partial — "kleine LA I" is a small unit at the **flotatie tank** that overflows at 90-92 cm; strongly implies **LA = a launder/overflow weir or level-controlled trough (likely "Lamellenafscheider" or overflow launder)** at the flotation tank. Row 16 confirms LA I is at flotatie tank 3b. Not spelled out here; still [unsure] on the acronym expansion.
- **Q15 (extruder temperature zones before/after laserfilter, why zone 3 = 175):** CONFIRMED numeric sequence for BOTH 3a and 3b: **200-235-175-240-245-250-255 °C** (rows 23 & 24), labelled **"Temperaturen zones onder en na laserfilter"** *(temperatures zones below and after the laserfilter)*. Reading with EREMA sheet 166_CeDo69: the profile climbs 200→235, DIPS to **175** (the cool "after melt filter" zone per EREMA — deliberate viscosity/temperature control around/after the laserfilter), then ramps 240→245→250→255 toward the die/pelletiser. So **zone 3 (175 °C) is the post-laserfilter cooling zone**; the low value is by design, matching EREMA TVEplus "After melt filter 180-220 °C" guidance (175 is just under that band). Zones 1-2 (200,235) are before/around the filter (backflush region), zones 4-7 (240-255) build melt temperature back up for pelletising.
- **Q16 ("Bunker speed 200-800" unit):** The form lists Bunker speed as **200-800 with NO unit given** (row 3), while the separate "Bunker height" is in CM (row 4, 100-130 cm). So 200-800 is a **dimensionless setpoint / rpm-like drive value with no explicit unit on the form** — most consistent with a drive speed (rpm or motor %/frequency counts) for the bunker (cutter-compactor infeed). Unit remains officially unstated on the doc → [unsure: likely rpm or a PLC speed count].
- **Q18 (Shredder rpm: lijn 1 32-50 vs Shredder 1 35-60):** This form gives **"Shredder 1 — Speed in rpm — 35-60"** (row 1). Combined with the earlier "lijn 1 32-50" figure, the two ranges differ (**32-50 vs 35-60**). Since this FORM-008 is a **line-3 checklist** yet row 1 is labelled "Shredder 1", the 35-60 here refers to the shredder feeding line 3 (Shredder 1 as a machine ID on line 3's front end), which is a **different machine / target than the lijn-1 shredder (32-50)**. So the discrepancy is machine-identity, not a changed target: distinct shredders. [Note: "Shredder 1" naming vs "lijn 1" is exactly the sim-vs-plant naming confusion — this is a line-3 form, so its Shredder 1 is on line 3.]
- **Q19 (Stortgewicht unit) — not on this form.**
- **Q28/TITECH (Q28):** row 2 "Tomra perslucht bar — Aflezen bij tomra's — 3-5 bar" confirms **multiple TOMRA/Timra units** ("tomra's", plural) on line 3 with compressed-air ejection at **3-5 bar** (air-jet ejection sorters). Supports TOMRA/TITECH air-blast ejection.
- **Q4 (Rafter 3b machine):** rows 13-14 confirm the **Rafter is on 3b**, has a **water level (93-96 cm, kept just overflowing)** and a **toerental of 50 hertz** (higher = imbalance) — so the Rafter is a **wet, rotating/vibrating water-bath separation unit** on line 3b (drive frequency-controlled). Make/type still not named here, but behaviour (water bath + rotational imbalance sensitivity) is documented. [unsure on make]
- **Q8 (does 3B have a bigbag station) — not addressed here.**
- **Compactor setpoint:** both 3a and 3b compactors run **123 °C** ("hoger=plastificeren" — too hot plastifies the flake). Kopfilter (head/melt filter) pressures: **3a 120-150 bar, 3b 140-155 bar**; must be swapped **≥1×/shift** or pressure climbs and melt loss increases. Heetafslag (hot die-face cutter) knife pressure **2,8-3,2 bar** (higher = wear). Antischuim (antifoam) dosing pumps 3a/3b must be on **Automaat** (automatic).
- **Q30 (units):** row 3 bunker speed 200-800 unitless; drive speeds elsewhere given in **hertz** (rows 14,15,17,18 = 35-50 Hz) — confirms line-3 conveyor/roll drives are specified in **Hz** on the operator forms (relevant to Snelheid/Vermogen hoofdmotor unit questions: plant uses Hz for VFD-driven rolls).
- Confirms line-3 sub-lines are labelled **3a and 3b** (parallel wash/extrude trains), consistent with sim naming 3A/3B.
