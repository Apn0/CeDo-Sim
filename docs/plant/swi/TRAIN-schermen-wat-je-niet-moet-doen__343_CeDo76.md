## Title (NL)
**"Wat je niet moet doen met schermen:"** — with "niet moet doen" emphasized in red. *(What you must NOT do with the screens / filter meshes [laserfilter zeefplaten].)*

## Content — photo grid (5 photos, no captions)
A montage of damaged / mishandled laserfilter screen plates (zeefplaten), illustrating bad practice:

1. **Top-left (portrait photo):** Stainless-steel piping/ductwork with a screen/scraper element resting against it — a screen laid down carelessly on/against pipework.
2. **Top-middle:** A dark grey/blue screen disc lying flat on a concrete floor with a metal scraper blade across it — screen dropped/stored on the ground.
3. **Top-right:** A round filter housing / bore seen from above with an orange-glowing element inside (looks like a torch flame or heated element) inside a dirty concrete pit.
4. **Bottom-middle:** A close-up of someone using an **open flame / gas torch (branderslang)** on a stainless conical screen holder — burning residue off a screen (a forbidden practice: torch damages/warps the screen).
5. **Bottom-right:** A round filter housing bore with a **flame/torch (blue-white jet)** being applied inside it — again burning out material with an open flame.

## Interpretation
The slide is a "don't do this" gallery: it warns operators against **torching / burning laserfilter screens clean with an open flame**, and against **dropping/laying screens on the floor or against pipework** (which damages the fine mesh). Companion to the "correct" cleaning/reuse guide (`GUIDE-laserfilter-zeefplaten-reiniging-hergebruik__303_CeDo75.md`) which describes the proper reuse/cleaning method.
