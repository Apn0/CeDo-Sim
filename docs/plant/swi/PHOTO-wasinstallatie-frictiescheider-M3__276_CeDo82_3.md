# PHOTO — "Wasinstallatie — werking frictie scheider M3" (Wash installation — friction separator M3 principle)

- **Source file:** `276_CeDo82 (3).pdf`
- **Type:** Photographed flip-chart / training poster page (Cedo Kunstofrecycling, "LEAN PRACTITIONER" badge bottom-right, dated **2024**). Landscape schematic + explanatory caption. No SWI header, no rev.
- **Title (header):** **"Wasinstallatie"** — subtitle **"werking fricktie scheider M3"** [sic: "fricktie" = frictie] (Wash installation — operating principle of friction separator M3)
- **Scope:** Explains how a **frictiescheider (friction separator)** works — the inclined perforated-screen dewatering/washing screw that separates dirty water + contaminants from the film before flotation.

## Diagram — labels (as annotated on the schematic)
An inclined cylindrical drum/trough (rotor with paddles inside a screen), tilted up to the right. Labels:
- **"E-Motor"** — electric motor at the top-right (high) end, driving the rotor.
- **"V-snaar overbrenging"** (V-belt transmission/drive) — belt drive from the E-motor to the rotor shaft.
- **"Paddels"** (paddles) — the paddle/flight elements on the rotor that transport and agitate the material.
- **"Product richting flotatietank"** (Product toward the flotation tank) — clean(ed) film exits the **high (top-right)** end and goes on to the flotatietank.
- **"Vuil water en folie vanuit pomp C1"** (Dirty water and film coming from pump C1) — the feed enters at the **low (bottom-left)** end, pumped in by **pomp C1**.
- **"Zeef"** (Screen/sieve) — the perforated screen on the underside of the rotor, running the full length.
- **"Waterafvoer+vuil"** (Water discharge + dirt) — dirty water and fines drain out through the screen at the bottom-left/low end.
- Flow arrows in the middle of the drum: **"Water"** arrow points down-left (water runs back down), **"Folie"** (film) arrow points up-right (film is conveyed upward). So film and water move counter-current: film up to flotation, water+dirt down and out through the screen.

## Caption (verbatim Dutch, bottom of poster)
"Het water en vuil wordt in deze frictiescheiders gescheiden van de folie. De elektromotor drijft de rotor aan. Aan de onderzijde van de rotor over de gehele lengte zit een zeef. Het materiaal wordt van beneden naar boven getransporteerd. Tijdens dit transporteren door de rotor wordt het water en vuil door het zeef afgevoerd."
- Translation: The water and dirt are separated from the film in these friction separators. The electric motor drives the rotor. On the underside of the rotor, over the whole length, there is a screen (zeef). The material is transported from bottom to top. During this transport by the rotor, the water and dirt are discharged through the screen.

## Answers to open questions
- **Q1 / component identity — frictiescheider working principle:** ANSWERS the mechanism — a frictiescheider = inclined screw/paddle rotor in a screened trough, V-belt driven by an E-motor, film conveyed up-and-out to the flotatietank while water+dirt drop through the bottom screen (zeef) and leave as "waterafvoer+vuil". Feed is dirty water+film from **pomp C1**.
- **Q5 / Q4 (water routing, block C1):** IMPORTANT — confirms **"pomp C1"** is the pump that feeds dirty water + film INTO the frictiescheider(s) M3. So block/label "C1" is a **pump** in the wash-water circuit (the feed pump ahead of the friction separators), and its discharge → frictiescheider M3 → (film) flotatietank / (water+vuil) drain. This clarifies the "C1" designation seen elsewhere as a pump station, not a tank.
- **M3 designation:** the friction separator shown is **"M3"** (machine 3 / frictiescheider M3). Ties to the Lijn-1 machine chain (frictiescheiders, intensiefwassers, MM1, ...) in `SWI-012_p1__274_CeDo111_2.md`.
