## Values / facts
- Lijn 3 has a **backflush filter** with (at least) two pistons — an **onderste/first piston** and a **bovenste piston** — each piston serving **two sides**, each side carrying a **brakerplate + 3 zeven (screens) + steunplaat (support plate)**.
- Brakerplate fixed by **3 bouten**, greased, tightened **hand-tight with a large flat screwdriver** (no wrench torque — melt-crusted threads).
- Used steunzeven go **in de oven** — a burn-off/cleaning oven exists for screen packs (consumables are cleaned and reused, not discarded).
- Top piston is mounted inverted: melt holes face **down**.

## Open-question hits
- Filter taxonomy for the sim: lijn 3 extrusion has BOTH a **backflush filter** (piston screen changer, SWI-025) and **laserfilters** (LF x - 40x, SWI-074). The backflush filter's screen packs (brakerplate/zeven) are a recurring consumable-change job distinct from the laserfilter disc change.
- **Q15 (zones before/after laserfilter):** no temperatures here, but confirms multiple melt-filtration stages exist in series on lijn 3, which is why temperature zones are split around filtration equipment.
