## Notes / extracted facts
- Laserfilter designation: **"LF 2 - 406"** — supports the laser filter (Britas-style) being a numbered unit; "2 - 406" likely filter nr 2, disc size 406 mm [unsure: interpretation].
- Filter change requires emptying the **extruder compactor** first — compactor is directly upstream of the extruder+laserfilter train.
- Two named screws at the laserfilter: **vijzel I (aandrijfzijde e-motor)** and **vijzel II (deurzijde binnenkant)** — each with its own dedicated tool. These are likely the contaminant discharge screws of the melt filter (lump discharge), relevant to the laser-filter/lump_cart model.
