## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q15 / laserfilter internals (major, for the user's laser-filter project):** The laserfilter is a **twin-chamber round melt filter** with an internal **schraper (scraper)** and **afvoervijzel (discharge auger)** riding on a central **uitzetschroef (jacking screw, "zonder flens" = without flange, socket 46 / sleutel)**. Turning the jacking screw inward drives the scraper + discharge auger outward for extraction. A **Ring** sits behind the scraper. Threads are copper-anti-seize sprayed (BOLL Cu-Spray). Scraper can seize to the auger — freed with a plastic mallet. This is direct real-plant evidence for the scraper/lump-discharge mechanism the user models. Socket size for jacking screw = **46 mm**.
