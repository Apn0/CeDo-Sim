## Values / facts
- The **heetafslag** (hot-cut pelletizer head) frame is held by a **centrale bout with linkse draad** (left-hand thread — tightens counter-clockwise, standard for cutter hubs so rotation self-tightens); seated by tapping the ratchet with a plastic mallet.
- **One SWI (SWI-55) covers lijn 1, 3A AND 3B** — all three extrusion lines have the same heetafslag arrangement.

## Open-question hits
- **Q26 (line naming):** official SWI title uses exactly the trio "lijn 1 3A-3B" — the sim's line names 1/3A/3B are the plant's real designations for the extrusion lines with hot-cut pelletizing.
- **Q5 (granuleerwater loop):** heetafslag = hot-face cutting, which is what the granuleerwater circuit serves (pellet cooling/transport water at the die face). This SWI confirms lijn 1, 3A, 3B each have a heetafslag, i.e. three consumers on granulation water. No routing details on this page.
