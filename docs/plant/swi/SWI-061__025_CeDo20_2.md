## Cross-references / open-question notes
- Nordson oven = **vacuum pyrolysis/burn-off oven** (consistent with Nordson/Schwing-type vacuum cleaning ovens for die/filter parts). Burn-off residue is "gruis" collected in a bucket → waste container. Sim: cleaning cycle produces a small solid-waste stream.
- Combined with p7 (waterfilter steps 23-26): the oven has a water circuit whose filter is checked after each run.
