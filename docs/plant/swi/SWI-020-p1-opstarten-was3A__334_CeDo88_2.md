## Answers to open questions
- **Q1 / Q29 (wash-line SCADA):** Startup of **was 3A** is driven from a SCADA/HMI mimic ("scherm naast was 3a") plus a physical console (lessenaar) with green start buttons — the **voedingspompen** (feed pumps) must show green both on button and screen. Corroborates that the wash line has a dedicated SCADA host (relevant to Q29 "3C = SCADA host for wash line 6" — here wash 3A has its own screen/console).
- **Q12 (LOTOTO):** startup gated on checking the **LOTOTO list** and walking a physical round of the wash installation.
- No answers to Q2-Q11, Q13-Q28, Q30-Q33 on this page.
