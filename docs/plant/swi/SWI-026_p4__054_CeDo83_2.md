## Values / facts
- **Reject balen** get a dedicated parking bay outdoors and are "later weer opgehaald" — sorter reject is baled and hauled away (sold/disposed), not looped back.
- **Invoerder** (infeed/shovel operator) has an **hourly** blockage-check round at the Titech–Tomra: **4 checkpoints** = Titech boven, Titech beneden, Tomra boven, Tomra beneden.
- Blockage response: stop the line, clear (vrijmaken); dedicated procedure **Cedo-PROD-SWI-032 "Verstopping Titech Tomra"** exists.
- Sorter access door may be opened carefully during production (with confined-space danger sticker present).
