## Answers to open questions
- **Q11 (LA1/LA2 meaning):** **LA2 = "Waterbak La 2"** — a **water tank/basin** in wash line 3a. Confirms LA# are numbered process water tanks/basins (waterbakken), NOT an acronym for a machine type. So "LA" denotes a water basin identifier in the wash circuit. (Answers the "what does LA stand for" question: it is the label of a waterbak/water basin; there is a La1 and La2 etc.)
- **Q5 (water routing — flotatie tank / ontwaterschroef / P1-P2 loop):** Concrete routing for was 3a **Water circuit 2**:
  - **Waterbak La2 → Pomp P2 → Flotatietank → Ontwaterschroef M3 (dewatering screw) → Glijgoot → back to La2** — a closed loop.
  - At the **Flotatietank** the stream is water+folie (film floats); at the **Ontwaterschroef M3** the water and film are **separated** ("gescheiden van mekaar"), film goes on (glijgoot chute), water returns to La2.
  - This confirms **P2 (not P1) serves the La2/circuit-2 loop**, returning to the flotatie tank line, matching the Q5 "LA2/P1 return to flotatie tank" question — here it is **P2** feeding La2's loop.
- **Q2 / machine numbering:** **M3 = Ontwaterschroef (dewatering screw)** on 3a's water circuit 2 — a concrete mapping of the M3 motor number to the dewatering screw. (Note: Q2 references "Frictiescheider M3" on 3A — same M3 number; here M3 is called the ontwaterschroef within circuit 2. Possible the M3 unit combines/adjoins the frictiescheider and dewatering screw, or naming differs by circuit — flag for cross-check with the 3A block-diagram docs.)
- **Q7 / flotatie:** Flotation tank sits between Pomp P2 and the dewatering screw M3 on 3a; film floats and is skimmed to the glijgoot.
- **Terminology for sim:** "Glijgoot" (slide/glide chute) is the film discharge chute after dewatering; "Ontwaterschroef" is the dewatering screw that separates water from film.
