## Notes / relevance to open questions — HITS

- **Q15 / Q17 (vacuum + "vocht") — HIT.** The extruder **vacuum degassing** stage (callout 4 on the extruder diagrams) removes **air/gas bubbles, moisture ("vocht"), and adhesives ("kleefmiddelen")** from the melt before pelletizing. This is why the process needs the earlier drying stages, and why residual "vocht" (moisture) is a monitored concern. Directly relevant to **Q17** (FORM-007 row 17 "...vocht") — moisture removal is a named process objective; the vacuum is the final moisture/volatiles extraction. Evidence: bullet list "Luchtbellen (gasvorming) / Vocht / Kleefmiddelen."
- **Extruder train sequence now complete (from these slides):** Compactor → Extruder screw (multi-zone, +laserfilter at zone ~3, 108/112) → **Vacuum degassing (removes gas/moisture/adhesives, this slide -4-)** → Kopfilter (screen-pack head filter -5-, 119) → smeltslang → pelletizer → dewatering/centrifuge → weegschaal (25 kg batches, 117) → silo/bigbag.
