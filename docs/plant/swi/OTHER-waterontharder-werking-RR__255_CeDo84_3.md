## Diagram — two configurations side by side

### Left unit: "Combi volume. Harstank staat in het pekelvat."
(*Combi volume. Resin tank stands inside the brine vessel.*)

Callout labels (top→bottom, verbatim Dutch with gloss):
- **Besturings unit.** — control unit (mounted on top, digital display shown)
- **Pekel leiding** — brine line (feeds from left side)
- **Harsvanger 0.2 mm.** — resin catcher / resin trap 0.2 mm (upper)
- **Dubbel werkende vlotter** — double-acting float
- **Hars van voedingskwaliteit.** — resin of food-grade quality
- **verdeel buis.** — distribution pipe (central riser tube)
- **Zout** — salt (the white pellets shown filling lower tank)
- **Kunstoftank met pe binnenmantel.** [unsure: "pe" = PE, polyethylene] — plastic tank with PE inner liner
- **Pekelwater** — brine water (bottom liquid layer)
- **Harsvanger 0.2 mm.** — resin catcher 0.2 mm (lower, at base of riser)

Layout: resin column (blue speckled) sits INSIDE the same vessel that holds the salt/brine (harstank in het pekelvat).

### Right unit: "Duo volume. Harstank staat buiten het pekelvat."
(*Duo volume. Resin tank stands outside the brine vessel.*)

Callout labels (verbatim):
- **Besturings unit.** — control unit (display reads "H2O" [unsure])
- **Pekel leiding** — brine line (long hose looping from separate brine vessel to resin tank)
- **Harsvanger 0.2 mm.** — resin catcher 0.2 mm (upper)
- **Pekelvat** — brine vessel (separate left tank with salt)
- **Dubbel werkende vlotter** — double-acting float (in brine vessel)
- **Hars van voedingskwaliteit.** — food-grade resin
- **Zout** — salt (in the separate brine vessel)
- **verdeel buis.** — distribution pipe
- **Kunstoftank met pe binnenmantel.** — plastic tank with PE inner liner (the resin tank, right)
- **Pekelwater** — brine water (bottom of brine vessel)
- **Harsvanger 0.2 mm.** — resin catcher 0.2 mm (lower)

Layout: separate brine vessel (pekelvat, left) connected by brine line to a standalone resin tank (right) — harstank OUTSIDE the pekelvat.
