## Values / facts
- Laserfilter unit designation "LF 2 - 406" → laserfilter nr 2 belonging to extruder/machine 406 (SWI title convention pairs LF number with a 4xx machine number).
- Full SWI-074 is 24 pages; laserfilter change is a major, tooling-heavy job (accu moersleutel, kunststof hamer, BOLA paste, koperpasta).
- Parts named: **afvoervijzel II** (discharge screw II — implies an afvoervijzel I also exists), **uitzetschroef** (jacking screw), toothed **vertanding** drive coupling.
- Bolt tightening: cross-pattern, 3 impact-wrench pulses per bolt per pass.

## Open-question hits
- Laser-filter project note (memory): the "afvoervijzel" (discharge screw) is the mechanism that pushes contaminants out — the lumps discharged to the lump cart come off this screw path. Two discharge screws (I & II) exist per filter.
- **Q15 context:** confirms laserfilters are numbered per extruder ("LF 2 - 406"); no temperature-zone info on this page.
