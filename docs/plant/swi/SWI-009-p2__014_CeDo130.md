## Notes / open-question hits
- **Q28 (TITECH/TOMRA):** Confirmed **two separate NIR scanner units exist: one Titech and one Tomra** ("Er zijn 2 soorten van scanners Titech en Tomra") — i.e. not one machine rebranded; the plant runs both makes side by side and calibrates both (this SWI covers both, Titech first). Titech UI: hardware terminal with membrane keypad, "main" button from hoofdmenu opens parameters. Cross-ref **SWI-011 = reinigen scanner glaasjes** (scanner glass cleaning is its own SWI). Calibration prerequisites: glass + belt clean, belt LOTOTO'd first.
- **LOTOTO** term used verbatim (lock out, tag out, try out) with padlock on the belt isolator — the plant's formal isolation term (matches padlock practice in SWI-039).
- Rev 04 and 2023 date: this SWI was actively maintained; approver name **Peter Scheffer** (Q-relevant for authorship realism).
