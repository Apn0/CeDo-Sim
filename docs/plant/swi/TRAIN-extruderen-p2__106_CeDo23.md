## Diagram description (embedded illustration)

Grey schematic of an **EREMA** extrusion line (the word "EREMA" is printed on the machine body). Left to right, with numbered blue callout bubbles 1–6:

1. **(1)** A coil/roll feed with a curved arrow at top-left, feeding a rising **inclined conveyor belt** (schuine transportband) up into the top of the machine.
2. **(2)** The **compactor** — drawn as an orange/red vessel labeled **"counter current"** with the Counter Current logo (EREMA Counter Current technology). Material drops in from the conveyor and is cut/mixed/heated/dried/compacted here.
3. **(3)** The **extruder screw / barrel** — long horizontal red screw section running along the base of the machine (tangentially / "raaklijn" connected to the compactor). Two small up-arrows above it near callout 4 indicate venting/degassing.
4. **(4)** Venting / degassing zone (two upward vent arrows) partway along the barrel.
5. **(5)** A **screen changer / filter unit** — drawn as an upright block with an internal rotor-like symbol (X shape in a circle), at the discharge end of the extruder.
6. **(6)** The **die / pelletizer head** — small block at far right where the red melt exits, with the melt path shown in red exiting to the right.

Grey outward arrow at the top-right of the compactor indicates exhaust/vent airflow.

## Caption text (verbatim, Dutch)

> "-2-
> De compactor, het materiaal wordt gesneden, gemengd, verwarmd, gedroogd verdicht en gebufferd, Vervolgens wordt de tangentiaal (raaklijn) verbonden extruder gevuld met heet voorverdicht materiaal"

**English gloss:** "-2- The compactor: the material is cut, mixed, heated, dried, compacted (verdicht) and buffered. Subsequently the tangentially (raaklijn = tangent line) connected extruder is filled with hot pre-compacted material."
