## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q15 / laserfilter (major):** Confirms the plant runs **at least TWO laserfilters — "LF 2"** here (tag **406**), plus the line-3 laserfilter in SWI-054. Internal construction detail: a **bi-metal** filter screen (thermal expansion holds it in the frame), a **spanring (clamp/tension ring)** with a wide edge to the door side sitting flush with the frame, an **afvoervijzel (discharge auger)** with a locking pin on the **E-motor side**, and a **schraper (scraper)** driven by an **uitzetschroef (jacking/set screw)** — this is the Britas-type continuously-scraped rotary melt filter that discharges "lumps" via the auger. Matches the user's laser-filter project model (rotary disc + corrugated discharge). The screen is a fine sieve (the "zeef").
