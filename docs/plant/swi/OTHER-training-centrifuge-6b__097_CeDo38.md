## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Machine make/type:** The **centrifuge is an EREMA** unit (brand confirmed by castings/logo). It is a mechanical dryer: granulate flung against the perforated drum wall, spun to remove residual water, auger/paddle transports it forward, then a **fan/blower conveys the re-granulate pneumatically to the weegschaal (weighing scale)**. Ties in with earlier EREMA-branded equipment in the plant (extrusion line likely EREMA too).
- **Q5 (water routing / crumb path):** Confirms the flow: **Ontwaterzeef (dewatering sieve, slide 6a) → centrifuge (dries) → fan/blower → weegschaal → (per ghost text) voorraad/buffer silo → extruder.** Residual-moisture removal happens in the centrifuge.
- **Q27 (silos):** Ghost text "opgeslagen in een voorraad silo" supports the buffer/supply silos holding dried crumb before extrusion.
