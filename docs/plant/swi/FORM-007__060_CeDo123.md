## Values / facts
- Rows 1–9 = **lijn 1** side (Extruder 1, compactor, vacuum zone, laserfilter zone, MAS 1 en 2, DRD, Heiz register, shredder). Rows 10–20 = **lijn 5** (each row prefixed "Lijn 5").
- **Lijn 1 setpoints:** compactor afzuiging nr. 2: 40% (higher blows film out, lower → moisture); compactor temp 108 °C (higher risks plastification); vacuum-zone temp 160–165 °C (deviation → vacuum floods); **laserfilter zone 165 °C**; MAS 1&2 filling 90% / emptying 60%; DRD MAS temperature setpoint ("Temperatur solwert" = German Sollwert) 30–40 °C (lower → moisture risk); Heiz register (heater register, German "Ein" = on) must be ON or moisture; shredder 32–50 rpm.
- **MAS 1 en 2** = two MAS units on lijn 1 (MAS = Austrian recycling-machinery maker; "DRD MAS" = MAS DRD dry-cleaning/drying unit — German-labelled HMI: solwert/Entleering/Heiz/Ein).
- **Lijn 5 machine park (from this form):** maalmolen (grinder, ammeter-controlled 130–270 A), **V10** unit with aflaat/puls/pauze valve timings (must be Automaat or "loopt V10 vol zand" — V10 fills with SAND, i.e. it is a sand/heavies discharge point, valve pulse 5 s / pause 45 s), shredder (fill level 15–25 cm, higher → brugvorming/bridging; run 4 s / pause 1 s cycle; longer run → slierten (strands)), **DSD schroef** 15% (off = more cost, too high = rejects/moisture), extruder schroef 50–70 rpm (too high → gasvorming/gassing), persschroef (press screw) 100%, vacuum −0,7 to −0,9 bar (less vacuum → more gassing).
- Reporting chain: operator → shiftleader → productie manager, every shift.

- **Q17 ANSWERED:** FORM-007 row 17 = "Lijn 5 DSD schroef | Uit=meer kosten, te hoog=afkeur/vocht | 15%". The word cut off is "vocht" (clipped to "voc" at the scan edge) — full ending: "te hoog=afkeur/vocht" (too high = rejection/moisture).
- **Q18 (shredder rpm 32-50 vs 35-60):** this form's "Toerental shredder 32-50 rpm" (row 9) sits in the lijn-1 block of a line-1-and-5 checklist, while "Shredder 1" (35-60) is attested as a **lijn 3** machine (SWI-027/033/034 "Lijn 3 ... shredder 1"). Supports DIFFERENT MACHINES, not a changed target.
