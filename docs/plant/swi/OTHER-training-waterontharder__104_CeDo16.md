## Content (verbatim NL + EN gloss)

**Sub-heading:** "Werking van onze waterontharders." (How our water softeners work.)

**Diagram (two softener types, cutaway):** Vendor is **"R+R Waterbehandeling & Service"** (logo bottom right).
- **Left unit — "Combi volume, Harstank staat in het pekelvat"** (combined volume; the resin tank sits inside the brine vessel). Labels (as printed, small): Pekelleiding (brine line), Besturings unit (control unit), Aanvanger 0.2 mm [unsure: "aanvanger" = strainer/collector 0.2 mm], Hars van voedingskwaliteit (food-grade resin), Verdeel buis (distributor tube), Zout (salt), Dubbel werkende vlotter (double-acting float), Kunstoftank met pe binnenmantel (plastic tank with PE inner liner), Pekelwater (brine water).
- **Right unit — "Duo volume, Harstank staat buiten het pekelvat"** (duo volume; the resin tank sits outside the brine vessel). Same labels; separate brine vessel (pekelvat) beside the resin column.

**Body text:**
> "Waterontharders werken volgens het principe van ionenwisseling. Dit houdt eenvoudig gezegd in dat het water door het harsfilter zal stromen, daardoor zal de kalk zich binden aan de hars. Om de hars weer schoon te krijgen zal men moeten regenereren, dit houd in dat men met zout moet gaan spoelen. Onze waterontharders doen dit volautomatisch. Hier is geen stroom voor nodig, dit gebeurt allemaal op waterdruk. Om de installatie goed te laten functioneren is een schoon waterfilter wel van belang, filter regelmatig reinigen. Voor de ontharder is stroom nodig (nog geen € 5 per jaar) om de klok en watermeter te kunnen laten werken."

EN: "Water softeners work on the ion-exchange principle: water flows through the resin filter, so the lime (calcium) binds to the resin. To clean the resin again it must regenerate, i.e. flush with salt. Our softeners do this fully automatically. No electricity is needed for this — it all happens on water pressure. For the installation to work well a clean water filter is important; clean the filter regularly. The softener does need power (less than €5/year) to run the clock and water meter."

## Notes / answers to open questions
- **Q5 (water routing):** The plant uses **ion-exchange water softeners** (vendor R+R Waterbehandeling & Service) to supply **softened water to the heetafslagen (hot-cut pelletizers)** — the sub-title "Water op de heetafslagen" confirms softened water feeds the granulation circuit, preventing lime/scale on the die-face/pellet-water loop. Two configurations exist (Combi: resin-in-brine-tank; Duo: separate brine vessel).
- Operational notes for sim/realism: softeners are **water-pressure driven (no pump power), salt-regenerated automatically**, need only ~€5/yr electricity for clock/water meter, and depend on a **clean upstream water filter** (regular cleaning). Ties to the granuleerwater loop (slide 6/heetafslag) needing soft water.
