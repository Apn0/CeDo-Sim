## Notes / answers to open questions
- Anti-fly / anti-insect nozzle at the **balenpers (bale press)** dispenses an **antigeurmiddel (anti-odour agent)** via a small dosing pump fed by compressed air. Pump is on a slide-out mounting plate held by wing nuts (vlindermoeren/vleugelmoeren). Connections: compressed-air line + anti-odour-agent supply through a reducing nipple (verloopnippel). Maintenance is a clean-pump swap.
- Confirms a **balenpers (bale press)** exists on the plant (relevant to bale handling / feed side).
