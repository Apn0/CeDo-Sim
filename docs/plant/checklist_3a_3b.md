## English glosses of key terms (first-use reference)

- Tomra perslucht = compressed air of the Tomra (TITECH) optical sorters; "Aflezen bij tomra's" = read off at the Tomra units
- Bunker speed/height = dosing bunker discharge speed (unit not printed for speed, presumably rpm/Hz-scale setting 200-800) and fill height in cm
- MAS bak 2 hoogte = MAS bin 2 level (height, in %)
- Vuilsnipper = dirt shredder/chipper; "lager=betere droging" = lower (speed) = better drying; Druk ... vol/leeg = pressure when silo full/empty
- Mengsilo uittrekschoef = mixing silo extraction screw ("uittrekschoef" sic, = uittrekschroef), % speed
- Rafter = the Rafter paddle wash unit (3B wet wash); "Nét overlopen !" = just barely overflowing!; toerental = rotational speed (in hertz, VFD frequency); onbalans = imbalance
- Flotatie tank intrek/uittrek rol = flotation tank infeed/outfeed roller (hertz); "kleine LA 1" = small LA 1 [unsure] (LA = pump/level designator, cf. Water_circuit_3A_LA1)
- Ontwaterschroef = dewatering screw
- Compactor temperatuur, "Hoger=plastificeren" = higher = plastification (melting) risk
- rand afzuiging EN reiniging = edge extraction AND cleaning (of the compactor rim); "Lager=minder droging" = lower = less drying
- Temperaturen zones onder en na laserfilter = temperature zones below (before) and after the laser filter — 7 zone values in °C: 200, 235, 175, 240, 245, 250, 255
- Kopdruk kopfilter = head pressure at the head filter (melt screen) of the extruder; backflush = automatic screen backflush; "veel smelt" = much melt (loss); "uitval vacuum" = vacuum failure/dropout
- Kopfilters ... 1x per dienst minimaal wisselen = change head filters at least once per shift; "Anders loopt druk op en daardoor meer smelt !" = otherwise pressure builds and therefore more melt (loss)!
- Druk heetafslag ... messen = pressure of the hot die-face pelletizer knives; slijtage = wear; 2,8-3,2 bar = 2.8–3.2 bar
- Antischuim pompjes op automaat = anti-foam dosing pumps set to automatic
